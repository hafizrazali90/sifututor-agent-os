## 7. Inventory and maintenance

The HTML index adds a searchable inventory of all Markdown, HTML, PDF, DOCX and TXT files under this concept folder. It distinguishes source documents, editions, restricted partner sources and historical samples. Render scripts, manifests, images and screenshots are supporting files, not extra reader documents; their counts are recorded in [DOCUMENT-INVENTORY.json](DOCUMENT-INVENTORY.json). Hidden metadata and symlinks are excluded.

Rebuild this index after adding, renaming or changing package files with `node production/compile-document-index.cjs` from this folder. The inventory records paths, sizes and hashes without embedding document contents. It is an index, not a portable ZIP or backup. No files were moved, no underlying claims changed and nothing was published or committed to compile it.
