Preserve teacher-only supplied iPads; parent and age-appropriate pupil access use their own devices. Younger pupils still benefit through classroom support and parent sharing without needing an account or device.

- **Proof hierarchy:** owner-approved problem/value direction; existing research with its caveats; clearly labelled proposed workflow/screen demonstration; existing Sifututor engineering experience; explicit validation needed before claiming product results.
- **Tone:** confident, concrete, respectful, teacher-centred and expert-led. No patronising description of Kota Buku. No national failure narrative as the opening.
- **Language:** BM and English, with one language at a time in HTML. Paired Markdown translations are source material, not simultaneous reader layout. Shared glossary and translation parity are required; separate single-language long-form PDF/DOCX editions are proposed in the new blueprints.
