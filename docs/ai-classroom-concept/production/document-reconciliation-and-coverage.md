## 3. Shared document contract

- **Central argument:** a connected teaching experience can reduce repetitive work and help teachers act on pupil needs, with suitable family involvement. This is intended value, not a measured product result.
- **External reader outcome:** understand what we recommend, how it would work, why Kota Buku has a meaningful role, what is proposed at launch versus later, and what the subsequent commercial discussion covers. Do not ask Kota Buku to design the product.
- **Internal reader outcome:** explain not just what was chosen, but the research, alternatives, assumptions, mechanisms, responsibilities, evidence and open checks behind each choice without needing the original researcher beside them.
- **Tone/depth:** plain language first; define terms; show examples; then add operational and technical depth where the audience needs it. Confident without guarantees. No arbitrary page count or forced defect quota.
- **Evidence:** use the existing researched corpus and communication-reference research to prepare the blueprints. This packet does not start a new research scope or freshly verify volatile facts. At drafting, populate selected claims from original sources and reverify within existing scope before external acceptance.
- **Languages and formats:** BM and English, one language at a time in offline HTML; separate single-language A4 PDF/DOCX editions proposed for long-form documents. Shared glossary and equivalent caveats. Product four-language requirements are separate from document two-language production.
- **Design:** layered sourcebook reading, navigable chapters, generous space and meaningful figures. No thick device borders, forced four-theme UI or cramped bilingual pages. Claude's preferred deck direction informs visual continuity but is not copied or modified by this task.
- **Identity:** Kota Buku proposed app, delivered by Sifututor, powered by Learnest Lab. Exact legal identity disclosure deferred. Preserve D1's platform/IP and market-right positions internally and for later contract reconciliation; do not import TEKUN's different ownership model.
- **Confidentiality:** external solution document figure-free; internal sourcebook also references rather than duplicates commercial figures. Public deck excludes private deal chain and negotiations. Internal does not mean unrestricted access to raw security findings or personal data.
- **Maintenance:** Hafiz owns direction; each accepted change updates this decision record, affected master sections and claim entries before regenerating editions. Review before circulation, after partner feedback, or on scope, evidence, rights, readiness or governance change. During drafting, check alignment at each chapter batch.
- **This packet's finish:** reviewed local blueprints and recorded decisions; no full document prose, generated editions or publication. Blueprint approval is still needed before full drafting.
