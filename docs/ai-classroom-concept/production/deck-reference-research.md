# Kota Buku deck: online direction and copy references

Research date: 2026-09-07. Local research/critique only. The reading draft, product scope and release decisions have not been changed by this research.

## 1. Question and conclusion

Hafiz requested online research into how comparable education/AI offerings explain themselves through decks: direction, copy, visuals and standalone comprehension. The intended audience is ordinary non-technical Kota Buku readers. The proposal must show the full teacher/pupil/parent experience, remain persuasive and future-facing, and be honest without becoming a readiness audit.

**Recommendation, not an external finding:** use a reading-first visual product proposal. Lead with meaningful human benefits, explain the broad offer, demonstrate concrete classroom and family moments, then show the delivery journey. Give Kota Buku's content role a visible place. Do not copy investor-deck conventions or turn the deck into a training manual.

## 3. Application to the current reading draft

These are recommendations for Hafiz's next discussion, not accepted edits:

1. **Retain the complete teacher/pupil/parent offer.** Use the overview to establish breadth. Let a small number of concrete scenes explain it; those scenes do not limit product scope.
2. **Strengthen the copy's human meaning.** Each headline should tell the reader what becomes useful or easier. The next short passage explains the action. Visible annotations explain the example and any material condition.
3. **Give Kota Buku value earlier context.** Seed the content-to-teaching opportunity near the opening, then explain its rights-dependent expansion in the dedicated content section. Do not imply that licence-dependent features are general-launch capabilities.
4. **Do not paste the reading draft directly onto slides.** It currently contains production-level explanation. The practical-use/governance and delivery beats are especially dense. Split into readable scenes where necessary; twelve story beats need not equal twelve finished slides.
5. **Avoid a tutorial feel.** Teacher review and paper capture should remain understandable, but the deck should explain the experience, not train readers through every button or troubleshooting step. Do not hide meaningful teacher effort.
6. **Use shared visuals with bilingual explanations.** BM and English each retain the action and meaning. Two long paragraph columns plus a detailed screen are unlikely to serve both projection and reading well; test shorter equivalent copy and, where needed, split slides. This is a layout hypothesis for visual QA, not a research-backed word limit.
7. **Make the delivery journey positive and specific.** Proposed general launch, guided full-RPH trial at the same launch, and later expansion can demonstrate progression without inventing dates or claiming automatic unlocking.
8. **Close with the recommendation and intended value.** Supplier experience supports the offer. It should not replace the final explanation of what Kota Buku, teachers, pupils and parents stand to gain.

## 4. Illustrative copy direction, not approved replacement

**Headline:** Ibu bapa memahami kemajuan anak, bukan sekadar melihat markah.

**English:** Help parents understand progress, not just marks.

**Explanation:** Guru berkongsi ringkasan tentang perkara yang telah dipelajari dan bahagian yang masih memerlukan latihan. Ibu bapa melihat kemajuan anak sendiri melalui telefon mereka.

**English:** Teachers share a summary of what the child has learned and what still needs practice. Parents view their own child's progress on their phone.

**Visible context:** Cadangan penyelesaian; ringkasan berkala dipilih guru. / Proposed solution; periodic summaries curated by the teacher.

The example keeps a useful benefit, an understandable action and the existing family-access boundary. It is not measured impact, a promise of autonomous AI-written reports or a new real-time monitoring feature.

## 5. Decision and next step

Recommend retaining the product direction while refining the copy and information hierarchy before visual samples. No new product research, feature, tool installation, proposal publication or vendor outreach follows from this communication research. The existing deck reading draft remains unchanged.
