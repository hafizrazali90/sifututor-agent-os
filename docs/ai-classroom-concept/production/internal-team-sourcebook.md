## 1. Understand the opportunity, people and proposal

**In plain language:** we are proposing a teaching assistant that helps teachers understand each student and the class, choose suitable support and follow up. The personal touch comes from the teacher; the proposed system makes learning evidence easier to use and helps prepare the chosen response. Generating material serves this purpose, rather than defining the product. This connects preparation, classroom work, evaluation and appropriate family involvement; it is not an existing app under a new name. Core evidence summaries, class views and rules support richer AI assistance in a same-launch guided trial with selected teachers, as explained in chapter 5.

Hafiz's brief combines teacher-workload relief with an ambitious classroom vision. If a teacher saves time preparing an exercise but must spend more time correcting it or copying records elsewhere, the product may not help. Conversely, a reliable learning record can support a teaching action that the teacher wanted to take but previously lacked time to prepare. Both efficiency and educational usefulness matter.

## 2. Understand what the research taught us

**The research question was broader than “which AI model?”** We needed to understand teacher tasks, curriculum structure, existing record systems, comparable education products, hardware limits, adoption and governance. The useful result is a set of design implications with evidence limits, not a list of impressive statistics.

| Research area | Lesson carried into the proposal | What the evidence does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher preparation | Guided AI drafting is worth evaluating as part of a complete teacher task. | A universal time saving or this product's effectiveness. |
| Existing school records | Add useful learning-process evidence; avoid recreating sensitive administrative datasets. | That no internal government learning records or APIs exist. |
| Curriculum | Preserve subject, year, standard and version so evidence remains interpretable over time. | That a curriculum label certifies an AI answer or makes scores comparable. |
| Classroom assessment | Structured responses provide a more checkable capture path than unrestricted handwriting. | That every paper response is captured correctly or every student has a device. |
| National programmes | Examine training, connectivity, adoption and accountability alongside hardware. | A universal single cause for failure, or proof that one vendor structure guarantees success. |
| Family portals | Curated information and deliberate permissions fit the approved direction. | A scientifically prescribed age threshold for every school context. |

The EEF/NFER December 2024 evaluation studied guided ChatGPT lesson preparation in secondary-school science in England. It found reduced preparation time and no detected difference in sampled resource quality. This supports testing the workflow, not transferring its result to Malaysian teachers, all subjects or student attainment. [Original report, executive summary](https://d2tic4wvo1iusb.cloudfront.net/production/documents/projects/chatgpt_in_lesson_planning_-_evaluation_report.pdf), reopened 7 September 2026.

The earlier research examined DIKSHA/Sunbird and Kolibri for content/offline architecture, Uruguay for sustained programme support, and several national device programmes for delivery difficulties. Those are dated research cases, not a controlled comparison proving what causes success. We borrow questions and documented patterns, not a claim that their results transfer. Their exact scale, legal outcomes and current status are not reasserted here. [Technical research §§2.1/12](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

Local and global product comparisons also serve different purposes. A vendor webpage can show how an offering is described, while an independent evaluation can test a defined outcome. Neither establishes our implementation readiness. Parent-portal patterns, teacher-generation tools and assessment platforms informed the six pillars; their marketing adoption counts, compliance claims and headline gains should not become ours. [Research §§4–6/8/10](../research-findings.md).

The communication research points toward benefits, concrete examples and distinct readiness stages. Hafiz settled the editorial choice: explain the experience first, then Kota Buku's opportunity and delivery. Prior-programme problems and policy alignment support the case rather than lead the pitch. [Communication references](deck-reference-research.md); REC-03.

## 4. Understand the whole teaching and family journey

Use this fictional fractions lesson with Cikgu Farah and Aiman Hakim in 4 Bestari to understand the relationships. It is not an approved curriculum example or a real student record. A lesson objective links preparation to the evidence collected afterwards; it does not itself validate a question or grade.

| Moment / actor | Input and action | Output and human decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher prepares | Select subject/year/standard; use a template; request draft practice. | Teacher-approved plan and questions, with source/version and approval state. |
| Teacher teaches | Use downloaded material, a projected activity or paper exercise. | Planned versus actual activity can be confirmed; showing a slide is not evidence every student answered. |
| Student responds | Supported digital response or a labelled bubble/tick sheet. | An observable answer; response time exists only if the actual channel measures it. |
| Teacher checks | Review uncertain captures, identity match and answer key. | Confirmed response/score and any override reason; missing responses remain missing. |
| Teacher follows up | Examine evidence and a traceable rule-based suggestion. | Reteach, adapt, gather further evidence or take no action; record the chosen response. |
| Teacher shares | Select progress highlights and approve a periodic digest. | Parent/eligible student sees permitted own-record information, not all working notes. |
| Next lesson | Review new evidence and prior intervention. | Refine the next plan; do not infer causal improvement merely because scores changed. |

The fallback matters at every step. If generation fails, the template and approved library remain usable. If a scan is unclear, the teacher corrects or re-enters the response rather than accepting a guessed score. If the connection fails, locally supported work must show whether it is saved or pending synchronisation. If family connectivity is unavailable, classroom teaching must not depend on portal use. [Product §§3–9](../product-feature-spec.md); [Data §5 measurement boundary](../data-collection-spec.md).

A later photo cannot reconstruct how long the student originally thought, what hints they used or whether they discussed an answer. These must not be invented as events. A mark is also different from a mastery estimate, which is different again from a teacher-confirmed formal TP judgement. Keeping those distinctions is the basis of trustworthy recommendations.

## 5. Understand the six-pillar product atlas

**Core** means proposed launch scope, not finished software. **Fast-follow** is the next stage subject to its prerequisites. **Later** requires further maturity or validation. The full-RPH, AI-analysis, teacher-assistant and AI-admin guided trial is a selected-teacher same-launch exposure rule, not a synonym for Fast-follow. Appendix A indexes every feature row; the profiles below explain why each pillar exists. [Product §§2–13](../product-feature-spec.md).

### Pillar 1: learning evidence

The teacher needs more than an overall score. Formative check-ins, structured marking and teacher-maintained mastery records connect responses to the learning objective. A standalone attendance register provides operational context but is not evidence of mastery. Inputs must be matched to the correct student and assessment version; the output is reviewable evidence, not an automated verdict. Handwriting and official-system sync are not Core shortcuts. Acceptance needs capture checks, teacher override and a clear missing-data state.

### Pillar 2: classroom activity

The teacher should be able to use material during the lesson, not only prepare it beforehand. Core polls/quizzes and downloaded resources support that purpose. The classroom route can use paper, a shared display or available student devices; it must state what is actually observed. Networked individual responses require connectivity and permitted access. Simple gamification and textbook-linked activities are Fast-follow, not reasons to assume behavioural tracking or content rights.

### Pillar 3: content preparation

Reusable RPH structures and AI practice address repeated preparation. Reading-level adaptation helps the teacher prepare a more suitable explanation. The teacher selects the objective and checks generated content, including answer key, language and difficulty. Full AI-RPH uses the same grounding service but starts with selected teachers in guided beta at launch. Four product languages are a Core requirement, not proof of equally good generated output. Textbook-grounded materials depend on rights/access; student-specific material depends on reliable learning evidence.

### Pillar 4: teaching support and personal attention

The central experience is **understand → support → follow up**, not “generate more content”. Help the teacher evaluate observed learning evidence for each student, recognise shared needs and decide what to try. The personal touch belongs to the teacher: a conversation, a different explanation, targeted practice or a class discussion. Content generation serves that decision. This is an intended benefit, not measured effectiveness.

Core student summaries and rule-based suggestions make the next action explainable. A suggestion should point to the observations that triggered it, and the teacher can reject it. These are not all generative AI functions. Core class views and rule-based flags/suggestions support every teacher at launch. Plain-language AI analysis, teacher assistant conversations and AI-written administrative drafts deepen this support in a guided trial with selected teachers at that same launch (D11, Hafiz, 2026-09-07). Templates and the curated parent digest remain Core. For example, shared difficulties could inform the teacher's choice of a temporary small-group explanation or class reteaching—not automatic grouping or fixed ability labels. After support, the teacher reviews new evidence without assuming it proves causation. Missing data must remain visible. Autonomous assignment without teacher choice is not the chosen model; a formal TP decision remains with the teacher. [Product §6](../product-feature-spec.md).

### Pillar 5: everyday efficiency

Communication templates and shared lesson plans reduce repeated drafting and support reuse. Approval matters: a parent summary is not automatically sent simply because a draft exists. Official attendance integration remains permission-dependent, and handwriting capture remains validation-dependent. Wellbeing/behaviour tools are not a headline promise or permission to collect sensitive family information. Their appearance as a Later research option does not override the data exclusion rules.

### Pillar 6: family access

Parents and appropriately aged students need understandable progress rather than a raw continuous feed. Core contains curated progress, attendance/announcements, age controls and periodic visibility on a separate web surface. Direct messaging is Fast-follow. Exact age bands and permissions, particularly the middle years, remain open; do not turn illustrative age examples into approved policy. No student devices are supplied. Acceptance must test own-child isolation, permission changes and understandable summaries as well as successful sign-in.

The intended scope remains Standard 1–Form 5 and government/private/international contexts. A rollout may require validated combinations of subject, year, language and curriculum, but no author may silently narrow the approved scope. Conversely, broad scope is not a claim that every combination is already validated. The coverage matrix must distinguish intended coverage, source availability, content review and actual release readiness.

Kelasapp's attendance/identity patterns and LLS's controlled parent views, teacher-led services and AI-output validation can inform design. Their existence does not prove code portability or shorten every differentiating workstream. [Product §1](../product-feature-spec.md).

That named product's eligibility and app-supplied local processing are different questions. This book does not add unverified RAM, speed or model-support claims. Device, fleet-account and distribution eligibility still need the existing checks; the proposed managed route is not permission to assume every account arrangement works. ## 7. Understand curriculum, content and AI quality

Curriculum is the organising reference, not just a label added to a generated paragraph. A record needs the curriculum version, subject, year/form, content standard and learning standard. Otherwise a revised standard may silently change the meaning of older evidence. The proposed hierarchy takes a standards-exchange approach; it does not claim certified interoperability. [1EdTech CASE](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/case), checked 7 September 2026; [Technical §4](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

The source pipeline is **permitted document → versioned content → relevant retrieval → structured draft → automated checks → teacher review → approved material**. Each step has a different job. Retrieval finds relevant material; validation rejects broken references or malformed output; the teacher decides whether the teaching content is suitable. Passing a format check is not passing a pedagogy review.

The Malaysian August 2025 preprint compared grounded and ungrounded Form 1 mathematics MCQ generation. Its corpus and automated evaluation were narrow, and it did not provide human validation. It motivates testing grounding, not claiming validated full RPH or all-curriculum accuracy. [Original paper §§3/6.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.04442v1), checked 7 September 2026.

Source rights are a separate gate. D4 removes dependence on Kota Buku textbook access for the initial public-DSKP approach; it does not establish unrestricted rights to every public document. Textbook retrieval/generation requires permission for that processing, not merely permission to read an e-book. Record what may be stored, transformed, displayed and retained, with withdrawal handling. [Product §§5/10/13](../product-feature-spec.md).

Quality review needs representative subject/year/language cases, answer verification, difficulty checks and teacher edit/reject evidence. Evaluate MCQ generation separately from full lesson plans. Capture failures also need separate measurement: accurate grading logic cannot compensate for misread handwriting. A foreign inference fallback is not authorised for identifiable work under the Malaysia-residency decision.

## 8. Understand data: what, why and who can see it

The principle is comprehensive learning capture within observable, approved purposes, not comprehensive surveillance of children. Every field needs an actor, source, purpose, access rule and retention decision. Automatic collection is not automatically justified. [Data §§5–8](../data-collection-spec.md).

| Record family | Examples and provenance | Key control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson | Class/subject/version, intended objective, actual taught standard, resource and reflection. | Separate plan from teacher-confirmed activity; opening a resource is not proof of learning. |
| Item/attempt | Item/source version, answer, score, mode, teacher approval and override reason. | Time/hints only where actually observed; paper scans cannot reconstruct them. |
| Student learning | Evidence history, teacher-confirmed mastery, interventions and access state. | Estimate versus formal judgement distinct; missing evidence is not a zero. |
| Class/school | Coverage, permitted summaries and intervention load. | Stage availability and access restrictions; standard IDs do not equate scores. |
| Teacher workflow | Feature use, errors, draft acceptance/editing, time proxies. | Not a work appraisal or proof of the policy teaching/admin time split. |
| Family interaction | Notice/permission state, curated views and complaints. | Own-record access, data minimisation and age policy. |

The proposed event envelope preserves event/message identity, time, actor, object, school/class/subject context, curriculum version, device/session/offline state and event data. It is informed by the previously reviewed Sunbird pattern, not a copied production schema. Stable event identity supports retry handling and audit; timestamps need the distinction between recorded time and later server receipt. The implementation must define those contracts rather than invent them from a presentation. [Technical §2.1](../technical-build-blueprint.md); [Data §5](../data-collection-spec.md).

Teacher G1/G2 access and student-record access are not the same rule. Individual teacher reporting is limited to that teacher and the immediate school leader; above-school teacher reporting is aggregated/anonymised. Student records have separate documented school/controller authorisation routes. Parents and students see only their permitted own-record view. Exceptional service/security access is minimised, logged and reviewed, not a reporting tier.

Do not collect household income, aid details, marital status, disability, health, religion/ethnicity, biometrics, psychometric results, location tracking or sensitive home-life notes merely because a source system has them. Even a contextual flag needs a permitted purpose. Do not feed teacher workflow data into appraisal or public rankings. Pseudonymised records remain potentially identifiable; aggregation needs suppression and linkage-risk checks.

### Access is specific to a purpose

| Reader | Student-record boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Subject/class teacher | Own students/classes. |
| Head teacher / GPK | Own school, for a documented duty such as intervention or moderation; individual access logged. |
| PPD / JPN | District/state aggregates; individual drill-down only under controller authorisation, logged. |
| KPM headquarters | National aggregates; individual access through a documented, authorised controller process, logged. |
| Parent / student | Only the permitted, age-appropriate own-record view. |
| Researcher | Approved pseudonymised extracts inside a controlled environment, not a general download right. |
| Public | Suppressed, rounded statistics; no public ranking of schools or classes. |
| Operational staff | Minimised exceptional service/security access, logged and reviewed. |

These are proposed student-access rules, not permission to bypass the stricter teacher G1 rule. Research requires vetted/trained people, an approved purpose, a controlled no-download setting, minimised pseudonymised data and checked outputs—the “Five Safes”. No direct identifiers or free-text extracts; no re-identification. Secondary use needs its own approved basis and applicable consent. A contract and technical controls must prohibit independent vendor reuse, commercial exploitation, teacher appraisal/discipline/ranking and feeds to e-Prestasi/PBPPP. These are proposed safeguards, not signed terms or settled statutory authority. [Data §§7.1–7.8](../data-collection-spec.md).

### The record has a lifecycle

A student record connects lesson context, observed item attempts, teacher-confirmed judgements and intervention history over time. A move to another school must use a controller-authorised transfer and documented portable format, not an unrestricted export or assumed KPM connector. Phase-1 generic export does not establish official format acceptance. Retention is set by the controller for each data class: the learning record is proposed to follow the student through Form 5 plus a defined period; raw prompts and workflow telemetry are short-lived. Exact periods remain open. Deletion follows controller instruction; end-of-service return/deletion and backup handling require an agreed schedule. Every individual read/export needs a tamper-evident audit trail visible to the controller, with its own retention decision. [Data §§5.3/7.6](../data-collection-spec.md).

AI quality and usage records also need purpose limits: prompt category, model/version and generation provenance, draft acceptance/edits, usage units and controlled cost-governance records are not a licence to retain raw prompts indefinitely. This sourcebook describes the record requirement, not commercial amounts or current model pricing. [Data §5.5](../data-collection-spec.md).

## 9. Understand safeguards and operating resilience

**In plain language:** someone must be accountable for permitted data use, and the software must enforce that account. Brand names do not settle legal roles. The intended controller/processor division requires exact entities, instructions, notices, retention and incident schedules before operation. [Data §7/§11](../data-collection-spec.md).

Identifiable processing must stay in Malaysia, including generation, OCR, logs and backups. A provider offering Malaysian servers is not sufficient proof that every subservice stays there. Map the actual path and test it. Recovery also needs demonstrated restores, not just a backup checkbox. Support access should be exceptional and reviewable; exported data must follow the same restrictions as the screen.

Incident handling needs a named owner, detection/triage, containment, evidence preservation, escalation, relevant notification assessment, recovery and follow-up. Exact legal triggers/deadlines cannot be inferred from a generic checklist. The prior review corrected overstatements about automatic vendor exemption and procurement exposure. Preserve the qualified treatment in Technical §15 and Handoff §13; obtain current authoritative/legal applicability checks before any commitment. This sourcebook deliberately does not restate unverified deadlines, tax liability or commencement as current conclusions.

| Safeguard | Evidence the team should obtain |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and roles | Controller-approved purposes, processor instructions and named accountable entities. |
| Family notice/access | Age/guardian rules, understandable notices, correction and complaint journey. |
| Non-punitive teacher data | Contract prohibition plus permissions, exports and audit tests. |
| Residency | Actual service/data-flow evidence across inference, storage, logs and recovery. |
| Accessibility | Web WCAG 2.2 AA target and applicable native assistive-technology checks. |
| Resilience | Restore/retry tests, failure communication and support escalation evidence. |

WCAG 2.2 is a target, not proof of conformance; native iPad behaviour needs its own tests. [W3C standard](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), checked during external production on 7 September 2026. None of these design requirements authorises collection, deployment or a legal conclusion on its own.

## 10. Understand onboarding and support

Adoption begins with completing useful work. A first-use journey should guide a teacher through attendance, one practice item, one checked response and a lesson plan. Guidance must explain what to review, not train teachers to accept every AI suggestion. Four-language materials and school champions are part of the proposed Core enablement approach. [Product §2.1](../product-feature-spec.md); [Technical §8.1](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

| Work | Proposed lead | Handoff / accountability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product onboarding and trainer guidance | Sifututor / Learnest Lab | Teaching tasks, known limits and technical help routes explained. |
| School coordination and training arrangements | Kota Buku | Participating context and logistical needs passed to the product/support team. |
| First-line enquiries | Kota Buku | Resolve known questions; escalate with safe, sufficient evidence. |
| Platform investigation/fixes | Sifututor / Learnest Lab | Diagnose, communicate workaround/status and confirm resolution. |
| Adoption/quality review | Joint | Review completed tasks, recurring problems and changes required. |

This is REC-05's approved proposal, not agreed staff or service levels. Do not assume IPGM/IAB or another institution has committed training capacity. Device/connectivity faults need a separate programme support route; the software team must not promise to replace hardware outside its scope.

An escalation should describe the task, expected behaviour, what occurred, environment and a minimised example. Do not request unrestricted student records as a routine troubleshooting shortcut. Where a problem affects a lesson, communicate the useful fallback first, then the technical status. Closing a ticket should mean the teacher can complete the task, not merely that a developer changed code.

## 12. Understand how usefulness will be measured

Ask whether the teacher could identify a need, choose a suitable individual or group response and review subsequent evidence—not merely produce another worksheet. Group-insight evaluation follows that feature's release stage; Core rule-based class views and selected-teacher AI interpretation need separate checks; neither is evidence of implemented capability.

Measure the work the product is meant to improve. A count of generated questions says nothing about whether they were correct, used or helpful. The proposed evaluation should combine workflow burden, teaching quality, adoption, family comprehension and operational reliability. These are proposed measures, not established baselines or promised targets.

| Measure | Definition / collection approach | Misinterpretation to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole-task time | Time from beginning preparation/checking/reporting to usable completion, including correction and re-entry. | Draft-generation latency as total teacher time saved. |
| Draft usefulness | Accepted, edited and rejected outputs with reasons, by task/language/curriculum. | Acceptance alone as correctness or student benefit. |
| Capture quality | Verified identity/item matching and unresolved/error rates in representative class sets. | OCR accuracy alone as reliable end-to-end marking. |
| Completed/repeated workflow | Successful real task and return use over an agreed window. | Login count as beneficial adoption. |
| Teaching follow-up | Teacher choice, evidence consulted and later observed work. | A score change as causal proof of the intervention. |
| Family comprehension | Whether the recipient understands progress, limits and next action. | Portal visits as understanding. |

| D7 | Rakaman pembelajaran menyeluruh mengikut tujuan dan tadbir urus; luarannya pelaporan dan analitik. | Bukan pengumpulan tanpa batasan atau tajuk aset data nasional. |
| D8 | Peranti dibekalkan kepada guru sahaja. | Web keluarga melalui peranti sendiri kekal; murid muda mendapat manfaat tanpa akaun. |
| D11 · 7 September 2026 | Paparan kelas, penanda/cadangan peraturan, templat notis/peringatan dan ringkasan ibu bapa untuk semua guru ketika pelancaran; analisis AI bahasa mudah, pembantu guru dan draf pentadbiran AI dalam percubaan berpandu guru terpilih pada pelancaran sama. | Deck dikekalkan; peringkat Susulan awal terdahulu diganti. Kesiapsiagaan, pemprosesan D7 dan semakan guru mesti disahkan. Beta RPH penuh D4 kekal. |
| C4 | Gabungkan pengurangan beban dengan visi enam tunjang bilik darjah AI. | Kedua-dua manfaat belum menjadi hasil produk yang diukur. |

Alternatif benar-benar dipertimbangkan. Menangguhkan semua rancangan pengajaran AI mengurangkan pendedahan tetapi kehilangan visi pelancaran; D4 menggabungkan templat untuk semua dengan penggunaan rancangan penuh berpandu. Laluan autonomi mengurangkan langkah guru tetapi turut membuang tafsiran yang diperlukan; model dipilih mengekalkan tanggungjawab guru. Menyalin kod aplikasi lain kelihatan pantas, tetapi audit bertarikh menemui ketidakpadanan dengan aliran sekolah asli/luar talian. Ini rasional, bukan alasan mengecilkan tawaran. [Handoff §7/§13](../CODEX-HANDOFF.md); [Produk §§1/5/6](../product-feature-spec.md).

Rangka kerja rakan menyumbang disiplin sumber, tanggungjawab, pencapaian, cabaran dan edisi selamat. Kita tidak mengambil penggantian keputusan automatik oleh penyelidikan, kuota kelemahan, kewajipan tujuh dokumen, penyembunyian jenama teknologi, integrasi rasmi yang diandaikan atau pemotongan AI automatik. Pakej diselaraskan merangkumi subjek berguna dalam laluan bacaan dan peringkat komersial berasingan. [Semakan rangka kerja](partner-framework-review.md); [REC-01–REC-07](document-reconciliation-and-coverage.md).

## 4. Fahami perjalanan penuh pengajaran dan keluarga

Gunakan pelajaran pecahan rekaan bersama Cikgu Farah dan Aiman Hakim dalam 4 Bestari untuk memahami hubungannya. Ia bukan contoh kurikulum diluluskan atau rekod sebenar. Objektif menghubungkan persediaan dengan bukti selepas itu; ia tidak mengesahkan soalan atau gred dengan sendiri.

| Masa / pelaku | Input dan tindakan | Hasil dan keputusan manusia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guru menyediakan | Pilih subjek/tahun/standard; templat; minta draf latihan. | Rancangan/soalan diluluskan guru dengan sumber/versi dan status kelulusan. |
| Guru mengajar | Sumber dimuat turun, aktiviti dipaparkan atau latihan kertas. | Aktiviti dirancang/sebenar disahkan; memaparkan slaid bukan bukti semua murid menjawab. |
| Murid menjawab | Respons digital disokong atau helaian bulatan/tanda berlabel. | Jawapan diperhatikan; masa respons hanya jika saluran benar-benar mengukurnya. |
| Guru menyemak | Semak rakaman kabur, identiti dan skema. | Respons/markah disahkan dan sebab pembetulan; respons tiada kekal tiada. |
| Guru membuat susulan | Teliti bukti dan cadangan peraturan yang boleh dijejaki. | Ajar semula, sesuaikan, kumpul bukti atau tiada tindakan; rekod pilihan. |
| Guru berkongsi | Pilih sorotan kemajuan dan luluskan ringkasan berkala. | Ibu bapa/murid layak melihat maklumat sendiri dibenarkan, bukan semua nota kerja. |
| Pelajaran seterusnya | Semak bukti baharu dan intervensi lalu. | Perhalus rancangan; perubahan markah sahaja bukan bukti sebab-akibat. |

Laluan alternatif penting pada setiap langkah. Jika janaan gagal, templat dan pustaka diluluskan masih berguna. Jika imbasan kabur, guru membetulkan atau memasukkan respons, bukan menerima markah tekaan. Jika sambungan gagal, tugasan setempat menunjukkan status disimpan atau menunggu penyegerakan. Jika akses rumah tiada, pengajaran kelas tidak boleh bergantung pada portal. [Produk §§3–9](../product-feature-spec.md); [Batasan ukuran Data §5](../data-collection-spec.md).

Foto yang diambil kemudian tidak boleh membina semula masa berfikir, petunjuk yang digunakan atau perbincangan murid. Peristiwa ini tidak boleh direka. Markah, anggaran penguasaan dan TP rasmi disahkan guru juga berbeza. Perbezaan ini asas cadangan yang boleh dipercayai.

## 5. Fahami atlas produk enam tunjang

**Teras** ialah skop cadangan pelancaran, bukan perisian siap. **Susulan awal** tertakluk prasyaratnya. **Kemudian** memerlukan kematangan/pengesahan tambahan. Percubaan berpandu RPH penuh, analisis AI, pembantu guru dan draf pentadbiran AI ialah akses guru terpilih pada pelancaran sama, bukan sinonim Susulan awal. Lampiran A mengindeks setiap baris ciri; profil ini menerangkan sebab setiap tunjang wujud. [Produk §§2–13](../product-feature-spec.md).

### Tunjang 1: bukti pembelajaran

Guru memerlukan lebih daripada markah keseluruhan. Semakan formatif, pemarkahan berstruktur dan rekod penguasaan oleh guru menghubungkan respons dengan objektif. Daftar kehadiran berasingan menyediakan konteks operasi, bukan bukti penguasaan. Input mesti dipadankan dengan murid dan versi pentaksiran betul; hasilnya bukti boleh disemak, bukan keputusan automatik. Tulisan tangan dan penyegerakan rasmi bukan jalan pintas Teras. Penerimaan memerlukan semakan rakaman, pembetulan guru dan status data tiada yang jelas.

### Tunjang 2: aktiviti kelas

Guru perlu menggunakan bahan semasa pelajaran, bukan hanya menyediakannya. Tinjauan/kuiz Teras dan sumber dimuat turun menyokong tujuan itu. Kaedah kelas boleh menggunakan kertas, paparan bersama atau peranti murid tersedia; nyatakan perkara yang benar-benar diperhatikan. Respons individu melalui rangkaian memerlukan sambungan dan akses dibenarkan. Permainan ringkas dan aktiviti buku teks ialah Susulan awal, bukan alasan mengandaikan penjejakan tingkah laku atau hak kandungan.

### Tunjang 3: persediaan kandungan

Struktur RPH boleh guna semula dan latihan AI menangani persediaan berulang. Penyesuaian aras bacaan membantu guru menyediakan penerangan sesuai. Guru memilih objektif dan menyemak kandungan, termasuk skema, bahasa dan kesukaran. RPH penuh menggunakan perkhidmatan rujukan sama tetapi bermula dengan guru terpilih dalam beta berpandu ketika pelancaran. Empat bahasa ialah keperluan Teras, bukan bukti kualiti setara. Bahan buku teks memerlukan hak/akses; bahan khusus murid memerlukan bukti pembelajaran boleh dipercayai.

### Tunjang 4: sokongan pengajaran dan perhatian peribadi

Pengalaman utama ialah **fahami → bantu → susuli**, bukan “jana lebih banyak bahan”. Bantu guru menilai bukti pembelajaran setiap murid yang diperhatikan, mengenali keperluan bersama dan memilih tindakan. Sentuhan peribadi datang daripada guru: perbualan, penerangan berbeza, latihan bersasar atau perbincangan kelas. Janaan bahan menyokong keputusan itu. Ini manfaat yang dituju, bukan keberkesanan diukur.

Ringkasan murid dan cadangan peraturan Teras menjadikan tindakan seterusnya boleh diterangkan. Cadangan perlu menunjuk pemerhatian yang mencetuskannya dan boleh ditolak guru. Tidak semua fungsi ini ialah AI generatif. Paparan kelas serta penanda/cadangan berasaskan peraturan ialah Teras untuk semua guru ketika pelancaran. Analisis AI dalam bahasa mudah, perbualan pembantu AI untuk guru dan draf pentadbiran janaan AI memperdalam sokongan melalui percubaan berpandu bersama guru terpilih pada pelancaran sama (D11, Hafiz, 2026-09-07). Templat dan ringkasan ibu bapa kekal Teras. Contohnya, kesukaran bersama boleh membantu guru memilih penerangan kumpulan kecil sementara atau pengajaran semula kelas—bukan pengelompokan automatik atau label kebolehan tetap. Selepas bantuan, guru menyemak bukti baharu tanpa menganggapnya membuktikan sebab perubahan. Kekurangan data mesti jelas. Penugasan autonomi tanpa pilihan guru bukan model dipilih; TP rasmi kekal keputusan guru. [Produk §6](../product-feature-spec.md).

### Tunjang 5: kecekapan harian

Templat komunikasi dan rancangan bersama mengurangkan draf berulang serta menyokong guna semula. Kelulusan penting: ringkasan keluarga tidak dihantar automatik hanya kerana draf wujud. Integrasi kehadiran rasmi bergantung kebenaran dan rakaman tulisan tangan bergantung pengesahan. Alat kesejahteraan/tingkah laku bukan janji utama atau kebenaran mengumpul maklumat keluarga sensitif. Penyenaraiannya sebagai pilihan kajian Kemudian tidak mengatasi pengecualian data.

### Tunjang 6: akses keluarga

Ibu bapa dan murid sesuai umur memerlukan kemajuan yang difahami, bukan aliran mentah berterusan. Teras merangkumi kemajuan terpilih, kehadiran/pengumuman, kawalan umur dan paparan berkala melalui web berasingan. Pemesejan terus ialah Susulan awal. Julat umur/kebenaran tepat, khususnya tahun pertengahan, masih terbuka; jangan jadikan contoh umur sebagai dasar diluluskan. Tiada peranti murid dibekalkan. Penerimaan perlu menguji pengasingan anak sendiri, perubahan kebenaran dan kefahaman ringkasan, bukan log masuk sahaja.

Skop kekal Tahun 1–Tingkatan 5 dan konteks kerajaan/swasta/antarabangsa. Pelaksanaan mungkin memerlukan gabungan subjek, tahun, bahasa dan kurikulum yang disahkan, tetapi penulis tidak boleh mengecilkan skop tanpa keputusan. Sebaliknya, skop luas bukan dakwaan semua gabungan telah diuji. Matriks liputan perlu membezakan sasaran, sumber tersedia, semakan kandungan dan kesiapsiagaan pelepasan.

Kewujudannya tidak membuktikan kod boleh dipindah atau memendekkan semua pembangunan penting. [Produk §1](../product-feature-spec.md).

Kelayakan produk bernama itu dan pemprosesan setempat dalam aplikasi ialah soalan berbeza. Buku ini tidak menambah dakwaan RAM, kelajuan atau sokongan model belum disahkan. Kelayakan peranti, akaun armada dan pengedaran masih memerlukan semakan sedia ada; laluan terurus bukan kebenaran mengandaikan semua susunan akaun berfungsi. [Teknikal §§3/6/7](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

## 7. Fahami kurikulum, kandungan dan kualiti AI

Kurikulum ialah rujukan penyusunan, bukan label selepas perenggan dijana. Rekod memerlukan versi kurikulum, subjek, tahun/tingkatan, standard kandungan dan standard pembelajaran. Jika tidak, perubahan standard boleh mengubah makna bukti lama tanpa disedari. Hierarki mengambil pendekatan pertukaran standard; ia bukan dakwaan interoperabiliti diperakui. [1EdTech CASE](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/case), disemak 7 September 2026; [Teknikal §4](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

Aliran sumber ialah **dokumen dibenarkan → kandungan berversi → carian berkaitan → draf berstruktur → semakan automatik → semakan guru → bahan diluluskan**. Setiap langkah mempunyai tugas berbeza. Carian memilih bahan, pengesahan menolak rujukan rosak atau format salah, dan guru menentukan kesesuaian pengajaran. Lulus semakan format bukan lulus semakan pedagogi.

Pracetak Malaysia Ogos 2025 membandingkan janaan aneka pilihan Matematik Tingkatan 1 dengan dan tanpa rujukan. Korpus serta penilaian automatiknya sempit dan tiada pengesahan manusia. Ia mendorong pengujian rujukan, bukan dakwaan RPH penuh atau ketepatan seluruh kurikulum disahkan. [Kertas asal §§3/6.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.04442v1), disemak 7 September 2026.

Hak sumber ialah syarat berasingan. D4 menghapuskan pergantungan akses buku teks Kota Buku bagi pendekatan DSKP awam awal; ia tidak menetapkan hak tanpa batasan untuk setiap dokumen awam. Carian/janaan buku teks memerlukan kebenaran pemprosesan itu, bukan sekadar membaca e-buku. Rekodkan perkara yang boleh disimpan, diubah, dipaparkan dan dikekalkan, termasuk penarikan sumber. [Produk §§5/10/13](../product-feature-spec.md).

Semakan kualiti memerlukan contoh mewakili subjek/tahun/bahasa, pengesahan jawapan, kesukaran dan bukti sunting/tolak oleh guru. Nilai janaan soalan berasingan daripada rancangan penuh. Kegagalan rakaman juga diukur berasingan: logik pemarkahan tepat tidak membetulkan tulisan tersalah baca. Laluan alternatif inferens luar negara tidak dibenarkan untuk kerja dikenal pasti di bawah keputusan residensi Malaysia.

## 8. Fahami data: apa, mengapa dan siapa melihatnya

Prinsipnya rakaman pembelajaran menyeluruh dalam tujuan diluluskan yang boleh diperhatikan, bukan pengawasan menyeluruh kanak-kanak. Setiap medan memerlukan pelaku, sumber, tujuan, akses dan keputusan penyimpanan. Pengumpulan automatik tidak semestinya wajar. [Data §§5–8](../data-collection-spec.md).

| Keluarga rekod | Contoh dan asal | Kawalan utama |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pelajaran | Kelas/subjek/versi, objektif, standard sebenar diajar, sumber dan refleksi. | Asingkan rancangan daripada pengesahan guru; membuka sumber bukan bukti belajar. |
| Item/percubaan | Versi item/sumber, jawapan, markah, mod, kelulusan dan sebab pembetulan. | Masa/petunjuk hanya jika diperhatikan; imbasan tidak boleh membinanya semula. |
| Pembelajaran murid | Sejarah bukti, penguasaan disahkan guru, intervensi dan akses. | Anggaran berbeza daripada keputusan rasmi; bukti tiada bukan sifar. |
| Kelas/sekolah | Liputan, ringkasan dibenarkan dan beban intervensi. | Peringkat ketersediaan/akses; ID standard tidak menyamakan markah. |
| Aliran guru | Penggunaan ciri, ralat, penerimaan/suntingan draf, proksi masa. | Bukan penilaian kerja atau bukti pembahagian masa dasar. |
| Interaksi keluarga | Notis/kebenaran, paparan terpilih dan aduan. | Akses sendiri, peminimuman data dan dasar umur. |

Sampul peristiwa yang dicadangkan mengekalkan identiti peristiwa/mesej, masa, pelaku, objek, konteks sekolah/kelas/subjek, versi kurikulum, peranti/sesi/status luar talian dan data peristiwa. Ia dimaklumkan corak Sunbird yang pernah disemak, bukan salinan skema produksi. ID stabil menyokong cubaan semula dan audit; masa direkodkan berbeza daripada penerimaan pelayan kemudian. Pelaksanaan mesti menentukan kontrak ini, bukan mereka-rekanya daripada pembentangan. [Teknikal §2.1](../technical-build-blueprint.md); [Data §5](../data-collection-spec.md).

Akses guru G1/G2 dan rekod murid bukan peraturan sama. Pelaporan individu guru terhad kepada guru itu dan pemimpin sekolah terdekat; pelaporan atas sekolah diagregat/dianonimkan. Rekod murid mempunyai laluan kebenaran sekolah/pengawal berasingan. Ibu bapa dan murid hanya melihat rekod sendiri yang dibenarkan. Akses khas perkhidmatan/keselamatan diminimumkan, direkodkan dan disemak, bukan peringkat pelaporan.

Jangan kumpul pendapatan isi rumah, butiran bantuan, status perkahwinan, ketidakupayaan, kesihatan, agama/etnik, biometrik, keputusan psikometrik, lokasi atau nota rumah sensitif hanya kerana sistem sumber memilikinya. Penanda konteks pun memerlukan tujuan dibenarkan. Jangan salurkan data aliran guru kepada penilaian atau pemeringkatan awam. Rekod berpseudonim masih berpotensi dikenal pasti; agregat memerlukan penyembunyian kumpulan kecil dan semakan risiko padanan.

### Akses khusus mengikut tujuan

| Pembaca | Batas rekod murid |
| --- | --- |
| Guru subjek/kelas | Murid/kelas sendiri. |
| Guru besar / GPK | Sekolah sendiri bagi tugas didokumenkan seperti intervensi atau moderasi; akses individu direkodkan. |
| PPD / JPN | Agregat daerah/negeri; butiran individu hanya dengan kebenaran pengawal dan direkodkan. |
| Ibu pejabat KPM | Agregat kebangsaan; akses individu melalui proses pengawal yang didokumenkan dan dibenarkan, serta direkodkan. |
| Ibu bapa / murid | Hanya paparan rekod sendiri yang dibenarkan dan sesuai umur. |
| Penyelidik | Ekstrak berpseudonim diluluskan dalam persekitaran terkawal, bukan hak muat turun umum. |
| Awam | Statistik dengan kumpulan kecil disembunyikan dan angka dibundarkan; tiada pemeringkatan awam sekolah atau kelas. |
| Kakitangan operasi | Akses khas perkhidmatan/keselamatan diminimumkan, direkodkan dan disemak. |

Ini peraturan akses murid yang dicadangkan, bukan kebenaran memintas G1 guru. Penyelidikan memerlukan individu ditapis/dilatih, tujuan diluluskan, persekitaran terkawal tanpa muat turun, data berpseudonim diminimumkan dan hasil disemak—“Five Safes”. Tiada pengecam langsung atau ekstrak teks bebas; tiada pengenalpastian semula. Penggunaan sekunder memerlukan asas diluluskan dan persetujuan yang terpakai. Kontrak serta kawalan teknikal mesti melarang penggunaan semula bebas oleh vendor, eksploitasi komersial, penilaian/tindakan disiplin/pemeringkatan guru dan saluran e-Prestasi/PBPPP. Ini perlindungan cadangan, bukan terma ditandatangani atau kuasa undang-undang disahkan. [Data §§7.1–7.8](../data-collection-spec.md).

### Rekod mempunyai kitaran hayat

Rekod murid menghubungkan konteks pelajaran, percubaan yang diperhatikan, keputusan disahkan guru dan sejarah intervensi dari semasa ke semasa. Pertukaran sekolah mesti melalui pemindahan dibenarkan pengawal serta format mudah alih didokumenkan, bukan eksport bebas atau sambungan KPM yang diandaikan. Eksport umum Fasa 1 tidak membuktikan penerimaan format rasmi. Tempoh penyimpanan ditetapkan pengawal mengikut kelas data: rekod pembelajaran dicadangkan mengikuti murid hingga Tingkatan 5 bersama tempoh tambahan ditetapkan; prompt mentah dan data aliran guru disimpan singkat. Tempoh tepat masih terbuka. Pemadaman mengikut arahan pengawal; pemulangan/pemadaman pada akhir perkhidmatan serta sandaran memerlukan jadual dipersetujui. Setiap bacaan/eksport individu memerlukan jejak audit yang menampakkan pengubahan, boleh dilihat pengawal, dengan tempoh penyimpanan tersendiri. [Data §§5.3/7.6](../data-collection-spec.md).

Rekod kualiti/penggunaan AI juga terikat tujuan: kategori prompt, model/versi dan asal janaan, penerimaan/suntingan draf, unit penggunaan serta rekod kawalan kos bukan lesen menyimpan prompt mentah selama-lamanya. Buku ini menerangkan keperluan rekod, bukan angka komersial atau harga model semasa. [Data §5.5](../data-collection-spec.md).

## 9. Fahami perlindungan dan daya tahan operasi

**Dalam bahasa mudah:** mesti ada pihak bertanggungjawab atas penggunaan data dibenarkan dan perisian perlu menguatkuasakannya. Jenama tidak menetapkan peranan undang-undang. Pembahagian pengawal/pemproses memerlukan entiti tepat, arahan, notis, penyimpanan dan jadual insiden sebelum operasi. [Data §7/§11](../data-collection-spec.md).

Pemprosesan dikenal pasti mesti kekal di Malaysia, termasuk janaan, OCR, log dan sandaran. Pelayan Malaysia daripada penyedia tidak membuktikan semua subperkhidmatan kekal di situ. Petakan laluan sebenar dan uji. Pemulihan memerlukan bukti pemulihan sandaran, bukan kotak tanda. Akses sokongan patut khas dan boleh disemak; eksport data mengikuti sekatan sama seperti skrin.

Pengendalian insiden memerlukan pemilik, pengesanan/triage, pembendungan, pemeliharaan bukti, eskalasi, penilaian notifikasi, pemulihan dan susulan. Pencetus/tarikh akhir undang-undang tidak boleh disimpulkan daripada senarai umum. Semakan terdahulu membetulkan dakwaan pengecualian vendor dan pendedahan perolehan automatik. Kekalkan huraian bersyarat Teknikal §15 dan Handoff §13; dapatkan semakan primer/perundangan semasa sebelum komitmen. Buku ini sengaja tidak mengulangi tarikh akhir, liabiliti cukai atau kuat kuasa belum disahkan sebagai kesimpulan semasa.

| Perlindungan | Bukti yang perlu diperoleh |
| --- | --- |
| Tujuan/peranan | Tujuan diluluskan pengawal, arahan pemproses dan entiti bertanggungjawab. |
| Notis/akses keluarga | Peraturan umur/penjaga, notis difahami, perjalanan pembetulan/aduan. |

WCAG 2.2 ialah sasaran, bukan bukti pematuhan; tingkah laku asli iPad memerlukan ujian sendiri. Keperluan reka bentuk ini tidak dengan sendirinya membenarkan pengumpulan, pelaksanaan atau kesimpulan undang-undang.

## 10. Fahami bimbingan awal dan sokongan

Penggunaan bermula dengan menyelesaikan kerja berguna. Perjalanan awal patut membimbing guru melalui kehadiran, satu latihan, satu respons disemak dan rancangan pengajaran. Panduan menerangkan perkara yang perlu disemak, bukan melatih guru menerima semua cadangan AI. Bahan empat bahasa dan pembimbing sekolah sebahagian bimbingan Teras yang dicadangkan. [Produk §2.1](../product-feature-spec.md); [Teknikal §8.1](../technical-build-blueprint.md).

| Kerja | Peneraju dicadangkan | Serahan / akauntabiliti |

| Sekolah dan urusan latihan | Kota Buku | Konteks peserta dan logistik disampaikan kepada pasukan produk/sokongan. |
| Pertanyaan pertama | Kota Buku | Selesaikan soalan diketahui; eskalasi dengan bukti selamat dan mencukupi. |

Ini cadangan diluluskan REC-05, bukan kakitangan atau tahap perkhidmatan dipersetujui. Jangan anggap IPGM/IAB atau institusi lain menyediakan kapasiti latihan. Jika masalah mengganggu pelajaran, terangkan alternatif berguna dahulu, kemudian status teknikal. Menutup tiket patut bermaksud guru boleh menyiapkan tugasan, bukan sekadar pembangun menukar kod.

## 11. Fahami urutan pelaksanaan dan kebergantungan

Pelancaran kekal besar: Teras merentas enam tunjang dengan percubaan berpandu RPH penuh, analisis AI, pembantu guru dan draf pentadbiran AI untuk guru terpilih pada pelancaran sama. Aliran pembangunan mesti bersedia untuk pengalaman itu bersama, bukan menyiapkan komponen terpisah lalu menggelar produk lengkap sedia. [Produk §12](../product-feature-spec.md).

Tiada tarikh tetap, bilangan kakitangan atau peraturan kohort seluruh sekolah baharu dalam buku ini. D6 menyerahkan penggunaan wajib/pilihan kepada pihak berkenaan. Penerimaan pelaksanaan dan model pencapaian komersial rakan mesti menggunakan definisi sepadan, tetapi buku ini tidak meluluskan jadual bayaran atau mengenakan syarat kontrak baharu.

## 12. Fahami cara kegunaan akan diukur

Tanya sama ada guru dapat mengenal pasti keperluan, memilih tindakan individu atau kumpulan yang sesuai dan meneliti bukti susulan—bukan sekadar menghasilkan helaian baharu. Penilaian analisis kumpulan mengikut peringkat pelepasan ciri itu. Paparan kelas Teras berasaskan peraturan dan tafsiran AI untuk guru terpilih memerlukan semakan berasingan; kedua-duanya bukan bukti keupayaan yang telah dibangunkan.

Ukur kerja yang ingin ditambah baik. Bilangan soalan dijana tidak menunjukkan sama ada tepat, digunakan atau membantu. Penilaian dicadangkan menggabungkan beban kerja, kualiti pengajaran, penggunaan, kefahaman keluarga dan kebolehpercayaan operasi. Ini ukuran cadangan, bukan garis dasar atau sasaran dijanjikan.

| Ukuran | Definisi / kaedah | Salah tafsir dielakkan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masa tugasan lengkap | Dari mula persediaan/semakan/pelaporan hingga siap berguna, termasuk pembetulan/kemasukan semula. | Masa janaan draf sebagai semua masa guru dijimatkan. |
| Kegunaan draf | Terima, sunting, tolak serta sebab mengikut tugas/bahasa/kurikulum. | Penerimaan sebagai ketepatan atau manfaat murid. |
| Kualiti rakaman | Padanan identiti/item dan kadar kabur/ralat dalam set kelas mewakili. | Ketepatan OCR sahaja sebagai pemarkahan menyeluruh boleh dipercayai. |
| Tugasan selesai/diulang | Tugasan sebenar berjaya dan penggunaan semula dalam tempoh dipersetujui. | Log masuk sebagai penggunaan bermanfaat. |
| Susulan pengajaran | Pilihan guru, bukti dirujuk dan kerja kemudian diperhatikan. | Perubahan markah sebagai bukti sebab-akibat intervensi. |
| Kefahaman keluarga | Penerima memahami kemajuan, batasan dan tindakan seterusnya. | Lawatan portal sebagai kefahaman. |
| Sokongan/kebolehpercayaan | Insiden menghalang tugas, pemulihan dan permintaan berulang. | Halaman utama sihat sebagai perjalanan guru berfungsi. |

Mulakan dengan tugasan garis dasar mewakili, kemudian bandingkan definisi sama semasa penggunaan. Rekod konteks tahun/subjek/bahasa, sambungan serta pengguna baharu/berpengalaman. Jangan terbitkan kumpulan kecil dikenal pasti; sekatan pemantauan guru tetap berkuat kuasa. Persetujui ambang, penilai dan eskalasi sebelum dakwaan lulus/gagal. Penilaian yang digelar bebas mesti berasingan daripada ujian vendor.

Hasil pembelajaran memerlukan reka bentuk penilaian sesuai melebihi metrik operasi. Penjimatan masa, penggunaan dan pencapaian ialah hasil berasingan. Hasil awal berguna mungkin persediaan dengan beban pembetulan berkurang; ia bukan bukti keputusan peperiksaan meningkat. [Penyelidikan §6.6.1/§6.7](../research-findings.md); REC-05.

Nilai jangka panjang datang daripada hubungan berguna. Rekod pelajaran/respons dipercayai menyokong pilihan latihan, kandungan dibenarkan menyokong aktiviti buku teks, dan antara muka disahkan mengurangkan kemasukan berganda. Tiada satu pun membuka semua ciri lain secara automatik.

## 16. Fahami cara menggunakan, mengemas kini dan berkongsi maklumat

Asas bukti bukan satu fail tanpa perbezaan. H merekod keputusan/pembetulan, P mengawal peringkat produk, T menerangkan seni bina, D mengawal data, R menyimpan penyelidikan, rakan memiliki angka dan buku ini menerangkan hubungan. Fakta diterima baharu patut mengemas kini sumber yang sesuai kemudian sintesis, bukan mencipta spesifikasi bersaing secara senyap.

Setiap dakwaan penting memerlukan ayat tepat, sumber/seksyen langsung, tarikh, status, pembaca dibenarkan, batasan dan lokasi penggunaan. Kekalkan D/G dan ID proposisi P01–P12. ID proposisi bukan hasil eksperimen. Laluan sumber setempat untuk edisi dalaman, bukan HTML awam atau luar terkawal.

Aliran penerbitan ialah **sumber → dakwaan bersyarat → ayat sesuai pembaca → edisi dijana → semakan kandungan/visual/pendedahan → keputusan edaran pemilik**. Bahagian dalaman disembunyikan masih berada dalam fail. Pembina perkongsian rakan didapati mengekalkan perenggan terhad dan teks ujian dalam skrip; ia bukan jaminan kerahsiaan. Bina kandungan pembaca yang dimaksudkan sahaja dan semak aset/skrip serta halaman kelihatan.

Kemas kini Markdown, kemudian jana HTML/PDF/DOCX. Simpan manifest sumber/pembina/hasil, log bertarikh dan semakan sebenar. Semak semula sumber berubah sebelum edaran/komitmen. Pembina berjaya bukan bukti kebolehbacaan; semakan kandungan bukan QA perisian; kelulusan arah bukan persetujuan pihak lain.

Struktur enam bahagian diluluskan Hafiz dengan “Yes” selepas penyerahan draf luar. Ini membenarkan penulisan dan semakan dalaman setempat, bukan penerbitan, penggandaan angka komersial, pelaksanaan kod atau perubahan dek Claude. Status edisi/semakan akhir direkodkan dalam nota penerimaan berasingan.

## Lampiran B. Kamus medan/peristiwa dan kuasa

Enam keluarga rekod dalam bab 8 masing-masing memetakan D §§5.1–5.6. ## Lampiran D. Sejarah keputusan dan andaian

Indeks berkuasa: [Handoff §7 dan pembetulan bertarikh §13](../CODEX-HANDOFF.md) bagi D1–D11/C4; [penyelarasan §2](document-reconciliation-and-coverage.md) bagi REC-01–REC-08. Perkara bab 15 masih terbuka pada 7 September 2026. Peranan/sokongan ialah cadangan diluluskan pemilik tetapi penerimaan pihak lain belum disahkan; undang-undang/hak/umur/format ialah semakan kuasa atau dasar belum selesai; kurikulum/rakaman/residensi/pengedaran ialah keperluan cadangan menunggu pengesahan; andaian komersial dikawal rakan dan belum diterima. Spesifikasi disemak pada 6 September; kelulusan REC bertarikh 7 September. Tiada yang dikelaskan sebagai fakta operasi terbukti. Indeks ini menambah, bukan menggantikan, penanda pengesahan sumber.

Urutan penting: penyelidikan awal → pembetulan bebas → penyelarasan rakan D8/D9/D10 → C4 gabungan → visual manfaat dahulu → penyelarasan rangka kerja → blueprint luar/dalaman berasingan → draf luar → kelulusan struktur dalaman. Arahan lama boleh tepat sebagai sejarah tetapi tidak lagi menentukan langkah seterusnya.

Jangan anggap “C4 terbuka” atau andaian jenama/peranti lama sebagai semasa. Jangan anggap batasan awal “tiada penulisan” masih semasa selepas kelulusan nyata. Sebaliknya, kelulusan menulis bukan kebenaran menerbit atau membina. Entri Handoff semasa, REC dan nota penerimaan menentukan batasan aktif.

## Lampiran E. Katalog penerimaan

| Semakan mewakili | Bukti sebelum mendakwa pelaksanaan |
| --- | --- |
| Sediakan bahan | Sumber/objektif betul, jawapan sah, sunting/tolak guru dan bahasa sesuai. |
| Rakam respons kertas | Padanan murid/item betul, tanda kabur selesai, tiada masa rekaan, audit pembetulan. |
| Luar talian/cubaan semula | Simpanan setempat, status tertangguh difahami, tiada percubaan berganda, konflik diuji. |
| Nasihat guru | Bukti boleh dijejaki, data tiada berbeza, pilihan guru, tiada TP rasmi automatik. |
| Kongsi keluarga | Penerima betul, dasar umur, rekod sendiri, kelulusan guru dan paparan berkala. |
| Sokongan/insiden | Bukti eskalasi selamat, pemilik, pemulihan dan tugasan guru berfungsi semula. |
| Percubaan berpandu RPH / analisis / pembantu / pentadbiran AI | Perkhidmatan bersama, guru terpilih, semakan pedagogi dan peluasan terkawal. |

Ambang, data ujian dan pemilik pelaksanaan bernama tidak direka di sini. Katalog ialah keperluan bukti masa depan, bukan pengisytiharan ujian lulus.
