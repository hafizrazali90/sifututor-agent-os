## 01. More attention to teaching, with families included

### BM

**Lebih tumpuan kepada pengajaran, dengan murid dan ibu bapa turut terlibat.**

Kami mencadangkan aplikasi Kota Buku yang membantu guru menyediakan pelajaran, mengendalikan aktiviti, menyemak latihan dan merancang sokongan seterusnya. Murid mendapat latihan yang sesuai dengan keperluan mereka. Ibu bapa mendapat gambaran kemajuan yang mudah difahami.

Dirancang untuk menyokong pengajaran dari Tahun 1 hingga Tingkatan 5. iPad dibekalkan kepada guru; akses ibu bapa dan akses murid mengikut kesesuaian umur menggunakan peranti sendiri.

### English

**More attention to teaching, with students and parents included.**

We propose a Kota Buku app that helps teachers prepare lessons, lead activities, check practice work and plan further support. Students receive practice suited to their needs. Parents receive an understandable picture of progress.

Designed to support teaching from Standard 1 to Form 5. The supplied iPad is for the teacher; parents and age-appropriate student users access the service on their own devices.

**Visual:** three people, three clearly labelled benefits around the proposed teacher app. Teacher: prepare and teach; student: practise and receive support; parent: understand progress. Draw supplied hardware only beside the teacher. Explain the whole offer before showing an individual tool.

**Internal source/status:** P01, approved strategic direction; handoff §§1/2/7, product introduction and §8. Outcomes are intended benefits, not measured results. Private/international applicability remains in the master scope; this deck addresses the Kota Buku proposal.

## 02. The next task starts with what the teacher has already prepared

### BM

**Hasil persediaan guru boleh digunakan semula sepanjang pelajaran.**

Guru memilih topik, menyediakan latihan dan menggunakannya di kelas. Selepas jawapan disemak, rekod latihan membantu guru memilih sokongan seterusnya dan menyediakan ringkasan untuk keluarga.

Tujuannya ialah mengurangkan kerja menyediakan dan menyusun bahan berulang kali. Guru tetap menyemak bahan dan membuat keputusan pengajaran. Pada peringkat awal, kemas kini dalam sistem rasmi sekolah masih dibuat secara berasingan.

### English

**What the teacher prepares can support the whole lesson.**

The teacher chooses a topic, prepares practice material and uses it in class. Once answers are checked, the practice record helps the teacher choose further support and prepare a summary for families.

The aim is to reduce repeated preparation and organisation. Teachers still review materials and make teaching decisions. Initially, updates to official school systems remain separate.

**Visual:** one readable sequence: prepare → teach → check → support → share. Carry the same illustrative fractions worksheet through the sequence. Label assistance and retained teacher actions; no numerical time savings, automatic official-system sync or seamless unimplemented integrations.

**Internal source/status:** P02, approved direction plus illustrative workflow; product §§2.1/3-9. This is a proposed joined experience, not a claim that all existing administration disappears. One lesson illustrates breadth without narrowing the product to that lesson or subject.

## 03. Teachers start with their lesson, not a blank page

### BM

**Guru boleh bermula dengan matlamat pelajaran, bukan halaman kosong.**

Guru memilih topik dan perkara yang murid perlu pelajari. Aplikasi menyediakan rangka Rancangan Pengajaran Harian (RPH) dan membantu menghasilkan latihan menggunakan kecerdasan buatan (AI). Guru menyemak, mengubah dan memilih bahan untuk digunakan.

Rangka RPH dan bantuan menghasilkan latihan dirancang untuk semua guru ketika pelancaran. Draf RPH penuh oleh AI diperkenalkan pada masa yang sama melalui percubaan berpandu bersama guru terpilih.

### English

**Teachers can start with a lesson goal, not a blank page.**

The teacher selects a topic and what students should learn. The app provides a daily lesson-plan template, known as RPH, and helps draft practice material using artificial intelligence (AI). The teacher checks, edits and chooses what to use.

The lesson-plan template and AI-assisted practice preparation are planned for all teachers at launch. Full AI-drafted lesson plans enter a guided trial with selected teachers at the same launch.

**Visual:** topic selection → draft practice and lesson-plan template → teacher-reviewed material. Use “Comparing fractions / Membandingkan pecahan” without inventing an official curriculum code. Explain the full-plan guided-trial boundary on the delivery slide and in its context card, preserving the finished deck's placement.

**Internal source/status:** P03, approved strategic direction; handoff D4; product §5/§12. Grounding starts with public curriculum documents, with rights/source verification retained. This is not an existing RPH generator or proof of generated-content quality.

## 04. Students can work towards the same goal with suitable support

### BM

**Murid boleh belajar topik yang sama dengan sokongan yang berbeza.**

Untuk latihan pecahan, seorang murid mungkin memerlukan gambar dan langkah yang lebih ringkas. Murid lain mungkin bersedia mencuba soalan yang lebih mencabar. Guru boleh menggunakan bantuan AI untuk menyediakan pilihan bahan, kemudian menentukan bahan yang sesuai.

Perbezaan keperluan murid menjadi sebahagian daripada persediaan pelajaran, bukan alasan untuk menyediakan setiap bahan dari awal.

### English

**Students can work towards the same learning goal with different support.**

For fractions practice, one student may need pictures and simpler steps. Another may be ready for a more challenging question. The teacher can use AI assistance to prepare material options, then decide which are suitable.

Different student needs become part of lesson preparation, without requiring every resource to start from scratch.

**Visual:** the same fractions goal with a supported version and a more challenging version; teacher selects, no fixed ability labels on children. Synthetic examples only. Do not imply an autonomous personalised course or automatic student-by-student generation from learning history at launch.

**Internal source/status:** P04, approved direction plus illustrative option; product §5 reading-level differentiation and §6 teacher control. Automatic history-based per-student differentiation is Fast-follow; teacher-selected adaptation is the story here. Examples require subject and language review before being rendered as teaching material.

## 05. Students participate without needing a supplied device

### BM

**Murid tetap boleh menyertai aktiviti tanpa menerima peranti baharu.**

Guru menggunakan iPad untuk menyediakan dan mengendalikan aktiviti. Murid boleh berbincang dan menjawab latihan di atas kertas. Jika paparan kelas tersedia, guru juga boleh memaparkan soalan untuk dibincangkan bersama.

Untuk latihan aneka pilihan, murid menandakan jawapan pada helaian yang disediakan. Guru mengambil gambar setiap helaian, menyemak padanan murid dan membetulkan bacaan jika perlu.

### English

**Students can take part without receiving a new device.**

The teacher uses the iPad to prepare and lead activities. Students can discuss questions and complete paper exercises. Where a classroom display is available, the teacher can also show questions for shared discussion.

For multiple-choice practice, students mark answers on prepared sheets. The teacher photographs each sheet, checks that it belongs to the right student and corrects any reading errors.

**Visual:** teacher iPad, optional classroom display and students with paper. A small capture sequence shows mark → photograph → teacher check. No supplied projector assumption, no student tablet fleet, no instant live per-student responses without a capture method.

**Internal source/status:** P05, approved direction plus illustrative implementation; D8; product §§4/9.1. Core example uses structured bubble/tick forms, not unrestricted handwriting recognition. The actual time and accuracy of this product's class-set capture remain unvalidated.

## 06. A checked answer helps the teacher choose what to do next

### BM

**Jawapan yang disemak membantu guru menentukan sokongan seterusnya.**

Aplikasi menyemak tanda jawapan aneka pilihan berdasarkan skema jawapan. Selepas guru mengesahkan bacaan, hasil latihan disimpan bersama topik yang diuji.

Jika seorang murid berulang kali menghadapi kesukaran pada topik yang sama, aplikasi boleh mencadangkan latihan berkaitan. Guru melihat jawapan yang menjadi asas cadangan dan memilih sama ada hendak menerangkan semula atau memberi latihan tambahan.

### English

**Checked answers help the teacher choose the next support.**

The app checks marked multiple-choice answers against an answer key. Once the teacher confirms the reading, the practice result is saved with the topic it assessed.

If a student repeatedly struggles with the same topic, the app can suggest related practice. The teacher sees the answers behind the suggestion and chooses whether to explain again or provide more practice.

**Visual:** class view and rule-based flags → one student’s checked practice record → teacher chooses an individual or group response. The teacher assistant may illustrate plain-language analysis in the selected-teacher guided trial, not general-launch availability. Keep the delivery distinction on beat 11 and in context cards, consistent with the finished deck. Note visibly beside the concept: “Keputusan pengajaran kekal pada guru / Teaching decisions remain with the teacher.”

**Internal source/status:** P06, approved direction plus illustration; product §§3/6/9.2. Assessment evidence is not attendance, participation or an automatic official mastery level. Structured-answer marking is answer-key logic, not evidence of AI understanding a child's learning. No claim of complete diagnosis from one quiz.

## 07. Parents receive a clear picture, not a list of unexplained marks

### BM

**Ibu bapa boleh memahami kemajuan anak melalui ringkasan yang jelas.**

Melalui telefon atau peranti sendiri, ibu bapa boleh melihat ringkasan kemajuan yang dipilih guru, bersama maklumat kehadiran dan pengumuman. Contohnya, guru boleh berkongsi bahawa anak sudah memahami satu bahagian topik tetapi masih memerlukan latihan pada bahagian lain.

Perkongsian dibuat melalui ringkasan berkala, bukan paparan setiap aktiviti secara langsung. Ibu bapa hanya melihat maklumat anak sendiri.

### English

**Parents can understand their child's progress through a clear summary.**

On their own phone or device, parents can view teacher-curated progress summaries, attendance information and announcements. For example, a teacher may share that a child understands one part of a topic but needs more practice with another.

Information is shared through periodic summaries, not a live feed of every activity. Parents see only their own child's information.

**Visual:** parent phone with a synthetic teacher-curated digest: what the child practised, progress shared by the teacher, attendance and announcement. No chat/composer implying direct messaging at launch; no automated AI-authored narrative or autonomous sending. Copy clarifies family visibility, not limited underlying collection.

**Internal source/status:** P07, approved direction plus illustration; product §§7/8/9.3; data §§5.6/7.3. The existing Core parent surface remains view-only and age-gated. Direct messaging is Fast-follow. Do not add a new parent coaching programme or homework-submission product.

## 08. Students can understand their own progress as they grow

### BM

**Apabila sesuai dengan umur, murid juga boleh melihat kemajuan sendiri.**

Murid yang lebih berusia boleh diberikan akses terhad untuk melihat ringkasan kemajuan sendiri. Mereka dapat membaca maklum balas yang dikongsi guru dan memahami bahagian pembelajaran yang memerlukan perhatian.

Untuk murid lebih muda, perkongsian diutamakan melalui ibu bapa. Akses disesuaikan mengikut umur dan dasar sekolah. Murid tidak perlu memiliki peranti untuk menyertai aktiviti kelas dalam cadangan ini.

### English

**When age-appropriate, students can also see their own progress.**

Older students can be given limited access to their own progress summaries. They can read feedback shared by the teacher and understand which parts of their learning need attention.

For younger students, sharing is primarily through parents. Access follows age-appropriate rules and school policy. Students do not need to own a device to join the classroom activities in this proposal.

**Visual:** clearly label two examples: younger student with parent; older student viewing their own summary on their own device. Do not silently age the student in the preceding lesson example or suggest every child receives a login. No student AI chatbot, unsupervised adaptive course or competitive rankings.

**Internal source/status:** P08, approved direction with age-policy detail still to be designed; product §8, data §5.6. Existing recommended age bands are not declared a settled national policy. This slide makes student agency visible without creating a new feature.

## 09. Kota Buku content can become part of everyday teaching

### BM

**Kandungan Kota Buku boleh membantu guru mengajar, bukan sekadar dibaca.**

Dalam pengembangan yang dicadangkan, guru memilih halaman atau bab yang berkaitan, kemudian mendapatkan bantuan menyediakan latihan dan soalan perbincangan daripada bahan tersebut.

Kandungan Kota Buku menjadi sebahagian daripada persediaan dan aktiviti kelas. Pengembangan ini bergantung pada kebenaran penggunaan dan akses kepada kandungan. Persediaan pelajaran pada peringkat awal bermula dengan dokumen kurikulum awam yang disahkan untuk digunakan.

### English

**Kota Buku content can support everyday teaching, beyond reading alone.**

In the proposed expansion, a teacher selects a relevant page or chapter, then gets help preparing practice material and discussion questions from it.

Kota Buku content becomes part of lesson preparation and classroom activity. This expansion depends on permission to use and access the content. Initial lesson preparation starts with public curriculum documents verified for use.

**Visual:** licensed-content expansion: page/chapter → teacher-reviewed exercise → classroom discussion. Label “Pengembangan dengan kebenaran kandungan / Expansion subject to content permission.” Use a neutral synthetic page, not an unlicensed textbook scan or a Kota Buku logo. Preserve the earlier fractions example where helpful.

**Internal source/status:** P09, approved direction conditional on rights/access; product §§4/5/10/12. Textbook-grounded generation and interactive content delivery remain Fast-follow. No claim of existing content rights, increased usage already achieved or exclusivity over curriculum.

## 10. Everyday use must feel practical and trustworthy

### BM

**Aplikasi perlu mudah digunakan dalam keadaan sebenar sekolah.**

Bahan yang telah dimuat turun, rekod kehadiran dan semakan jawapan pada peranti dirancang untuk terus digunakan ketika internet terhad. Penghasilan bahan baharu melalui AI dalam talian memerlukan sambungan.

Panduan langkah demi langkah membantu guru bermula. Sokongan Bahasa Melayu, Inggeris, Mandarin dan Tamil serta ciri kebolehcapaian termasuk dalam cadangan. Maklumat yang boleh mengenal pasti murid dirancang untuk diproses dan disimpan di Malaysia, dengan akses mengikut peranan dan tujuan yang dibenarkan.

### English

**The app must be practical in real school conditions.**

Downloaded materials, attendance records and on-device answer checking are designed to remain usable with limited internet. Creating new material through online AI requires a connection.

Step-by-step guidance helps teachers get started. Malay, English, Mandarin and Tamil support and accessibility features are included in the proposal. Identifiable student information is intended to be processed and stored in Malaysia, with access limited by authorised role and purpose.

**Visual:** three labelled areas, not a technical architecture: use prepared materials; get help starting; protect information. Include a readable paired caption: “Guru dan pemimpin sekolah yang menyelia mereka boleh melihat rekod penggunaan guru tersebut. Peringkat lebih tinggi hanya menerima ringkasan kumpulan tanpa mengenal pasti guru individu / Teachers and their immediate school leader can see that teacher's use records. Higher levels receive only group summaries that do not identify individual teachers.” Add “Untuk sokongan, bukan penilaian prestasi atau hukuman / For support, not appraisal or punishment.” This beat may need two slides for readable bilingual output; retain all safeguards rather than shrinking them.

**Internal source/status:** P10, approved design requirements, not completed compliance or service availability; product §§2.1/4/9, data §7, blueprint §§2/8.1/9.2. Malaysia-resident identifiable processing, language quality and accessibility require validation. No claim that public-cloud generation works offline, no student-record access restrictions accidentally replaced by the teacher-only telemetry rule.

## 11. The delivery journey brings the full experience into use

### BM

**Pelaksanaan berperingkat membawa pengalaman menyeluruh ini kepada pengguna.**

**Pelancaran:** persediaan pelajaran dan latihan, aktiviti kelas, semakan jawapan aneka pilihan yang ditanda, rekod kemajuan murid, paparan kelas, penanda dan cadangan berasaskan peraturan, perkongsian bahan guru serta ringkasan untuk ibu bapa dan murid mengikut kesesuaian umur. Guru boleh menggunakan semula bahan yang dikongsi dan menyediakan notis atau peringatan daripada templat untuk disemak sebelum dihantar. Panduan penggunaan, sokongan bahasa dan kebolehcapaian disediakan bersama.

**Percubaan berpandu ketika pelancaran:** draf RPH penuh, analisis AI dalam bahasa mudah, pembantu AI untuk guru dan draf pentadbiran janaan AI untuk guru terpilih sebelum diperluas.

**Pengembangan:** latihan daripada kandungan Kota Buku dengan kebenaran; semakan tulisan tangan selepas diuji; sambungan kepada sistem rasmi selepas mendapat kelulusan dan akses; bahan khusus murid secara automatik berdasarkan sejarah pembelajaran selepas pengesahan.

### English

**Staged delivery brings the complete experience into everyday use.**

**Launch:** lesson and practice preparation, classroom activities, marked multiple-choice answer checking, student progress records, class views, rule-based flags and suggestions, teacher resource sharing and age-appropriate parent and student summaries. Teachers can reuse shared materials and prepare notices or reminders from templates for review before sending. Guidance, language support and accessibility are included.

**Guided trial at launch:** full AI-drafted lesson plans, plain-language AI analysis, the teacher assistant and AI-written administrative drafts for selected teachers before wider release.

**Expansion:** practice from Kota Buku content with permission; handwriting checking after validation; connections to official systems after approval and access; automatic history-based individualised material after validation.

**Visual:** broad launch foundation with a guided-trial lane starting at the same point and a dependent-expansion lane. No invented dates, budgets, percentage-complete claims or promise that all expansion happens automatically after release. Preserve attendance in the launch illustration/captions if split across slides; communication templates and teacher sharing are explained in the reader-facing copy above.

**Internal source/status:** P11, approved strategic direction and preserved sequencing; D4/D6/D8/D11; product §§2.1/3-8/12. Launch progress suggestions are rules-based, not generative narratives. A staged delivery mechanism does not choose mandatory or optional adoption for Kota Buku/KPM. This summarises, not replaces, the full specification.
