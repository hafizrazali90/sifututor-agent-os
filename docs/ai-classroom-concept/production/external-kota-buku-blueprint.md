## 1. Document contract

**Working title:** More time for teaching: a proposed AI-supported classroom experience for Kota Buku.

**Primary reader:** non-technical Kota Buku decision-makers and programme staff. Secondary readers: their authorised education, operations, technology and procurement reviewers. This is a controlled external explanation, not the public deck and not the priced commercial proposal.

**Reader outcome:** understand the recommended experience without a presenter; see teacher, pupil and parent value; understand Kota Buku's proposed role and our delivery role; distinguish proposed launch, guided beta and dependent expansion; know what the later commercial package would address.

**Story:** benefits → show how it works → Kota Buku's opportunity → delivery and trust → subsequent commercial decision. Existing sources and labelled illustrations explain the proposal; neither proves measured outcomes. Product breadth stays ambitious; every material readiness qualification sits beside its benefit, not buried at the end.

**Outputs after approval and drafting:** governed BM/English Markdown, offline language-switching HTML, separate single-language A4 PDF and editable DOCX editions. No forced length. This packet creates only the blueprint. Actual export design and public/recipient release require later checks.

## 2. Editorial and disclosure rules

- Start each chapter with a plain-language answer, then one recognisable example and only necessary detail. Define RPH as a lesson plan and DSKP as curriculum standards before using abbreviations.
- Explain what AI drafts, what conventional software calculates, and what the teacher checks or decides. Do not call every useful function AI.
- Show an illustrative current workflow and intended improvement, with steps retained. Do not claim every teacher works identically, measured minutes saved, or elimination of official-system re-entry.
- Keep pupils and parents present throughout; supplied iPads are teachers-only. Proposed family access uses their own devices and appropriate access controls. Classroom benefit does not require every child to log in.
- Use “proposed”, “intended” and specific launch-stage captions naturally. All software capability remains proposed unless separately supported by dated implementation evidence.
- Retain the full six-pillar offer. The launch summary is not just two features; template planning and AI practice are highlights within the broader Core scope.
- No commercial figures, private deal chain, staffing budget, restricted security findings, speculative legal conclusions, completion percentages or national-data-asset headline. Describe reporting and analytics with the actual access limits.
- Sifututor fronts, Learnest Lab remains visible. Partner roles are proposed. No borrowed logos/assets without release rights, no implied Kota Buku/KPM endorsement, no invitation to Kota Buku to design the product.
