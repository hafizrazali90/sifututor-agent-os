# More time for teaching

## A proposed AI-supported classroom experience for Kota Buku

Delivered by Sifututor. Powered by Learnest Lab.

**Discussion draft · 7 September 2026 · Version 0.3**

Prepared for Kota Buku. Proposed solution and responsibilities, subject to agreement. The examples illustrate the intended experience, not an already-deployed product or measured classroom results. Commercial terms will be presented separately.

### How to read this document

Start with chapters 1–6 to understand the experience for teachers, students and parents. Chapters 7–10 explain Kota Buku's opportunity, delivery and safeguards. Chapters 11–12 cover relevant experience, evaluation and the next commercial discussion. The feature index and glossary provide a quick reference.

## 1. A connected experience, built around teaching

A teacher's work does not end when a lesson is prepared. The lesson needs suitable exercises, the responses need checking, and what students struggled with should inform the next lesson. Parents also need an understandable account of progress.

We propose a Kota Buku classroom application that connects those tasks. Teachers would use an iPad workspace to prepare, teach, review learning evidence and choose follow-up support. A separate, lightweight web experience would give parents and age-appropriate students access to suitable information on their own devices.

The aim is both practical and ambitious: give teachers more room for personal attention, not just faster content generation. The proposed teaching assistant would help them **understand each student and the class → choose suitable support → follow up on progress**. Recorded evidence and explainable rules provide the foundation; richer AI assistance would build on it in the stages described in chapter 5. Drafting exercises is one way to support a teaching decision, not the whole purpose. The teacher provides the personal touch and retains formal assessment judgement.

| Person | What the proposed experience makes possible |
| --- | --- |
| Teacher | Reuse lesson structures, prepare suitable practice, check structured answers and decide what to teach next from connected records. |
| Student | Receive clearer classroom support and suitable practice, including through paper-based activities without a personal device. |
| Parent | Receive a teacher-approved explanation of progress and a manageable way to support learning at home. |
| Kota Buku | Bring content, programme coordination and classroom use into a coherent proposed offer with Sifututor's product and technology delivery. |

This is a substantial six-part experience, not a collection of isolated AI prompts. It connects student learning records, classroom activities, content preparation, teaching support, everyday efficiency and family access. Chapter 8 distinguishes the proposed launch scope from subsequent expansion.

## 2. What would change in the teacher's day?

The useful question is not simply “Can AI write this?” It is “Which parts of this job can become easier, while preserving the teacher's judgement?”

The following is an illustrative workflow, not a finding that every school works in the same way or a measured time-saving claim.

| Task | Proposed improvement | What the teacher still does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare a lesson | Start with a reusable lesson structure and selected curriculum objective. | Set the purpose, sequence and suitability of the lesson. |
| Prepare different practice | Ask for draft questions or a simpler explanation tied to the objective. | Check correctness, language and difficulty before use. |
| Review structured answers | Capture supported multiple-choice answer sheets and check answers against a key. | Resolve unclear captures and interpret what the answers mean. |
| Plan follow-up | Review a student's recorded strengths and difficulties with a traceable suggestion. | Decide whether to reteach, change the exercise or gather more evidence. |
| Update parents | Adapt a communication template and share a curated progress summary. | Decide what is appropriate to share and approve it. |

The intended reduction is in repeated drafting, manual collation and preparing similar information in several places. It is not a promise to remove every administrative task. The first phase would operate independently of Ministry of Education systems. Generic exports can support review and record handling, but required official entry may still need to be completed separately.

We would measure the whole task, including AI corrections, capture checks and remaining duplicate entry. A faster draft is only useful if the final material is suitable and the overall workflow actually helps the teacher.

## 3. Prepare a lesson and suitable exercises

Consider Cikgu Farah preparing a fractions lesson for 4 Bestari. All names and records in this example are fictional. It illustrates the interaction, not a certified curriculum mapping, an official lesson-plan template or an operating app screenshot.

### Start with what students should understand

The teacher selects the subject, year and relevant learning objective. The application would organise curriculum choices using the applicable **DSKP**, the Malaysian curriculum and assessment standards documents. The teacher can then complete a structured **RPH**, or daily lesson plan, rather than recreate the layout each time.

> **Concept illustration · proposed general launch**
>
> Lesson focus: comparing simple fractions using equal-sized wholes.
>
> Opening: ask students to compare two pictures with the same whole divided differently.
>
> Practice request: draft a short multiple-choice exercise and a more supported version using pictures.
>
> Teacher check: confirm the pictures, answer key, wording and suitability before printing.

The proposed workflow is: **choose an objective → use a template → request practice → check and adapt → teach**. A shared lesson-plan library would let teachers reuse suitable structures and improve them over time.

### Give the teacher a useful draft, not a final answer

AI-generated practice and reading-level adaptation are part of the proposed general-launch experience. The teacher checks that an item has a valid answer, that its explanation is correct and that the wording suits the class. Unsuitable material can be edited, rejected or replaced with an existing teacher-approved resource.

Full AI-drafted lesson plans would also be built for the same launch, but initially exposed through a **guided beta for selected teachers**. This means supervised early use with quality feedback before broader availability. It is not a separate later build, nor a promise that every teacher receives full-plan generation immediately.

The initial grounding approach uses public curriculum documents rather than depending on Kota Buku textbook ingestion. Exact source-use terms still need verification. Textbook-based drafting would follow only where rights and usable access are established. Teacher validation of generated materials is required before launch.

## 4. Teach, collect responses and check understanding

The iPad would support the lesson, not require every student to own a device. A teacher could present an activity using available classroom display equipment, use printed practice, or allow eligible students to respond on their own devices where access and connectivity permit.

These routes produce different kinds of evidence:

| Classroom route | What it can support | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher-led discussion or projected question | Shared explanation and discussion. | Participation alone does not create an identified answer record for every student. |
| Supported bubble/tick multiple-choice sheet | Capture a structured response and check it against the answer key. | The teacher must resolve uncertain marks and confirm the student-to-sheet match. |
| In-app quiz using available student devices | Attributable responses where the student is appropriately identified. | Requires suitable access, devices and connectivity; these are not supplied to students through this proposal. |

For the paper route, the intended sequence is **capture → review unclear marks → confirm identity → check answers → save learning evidence**. This is structured marking, not an AI judgement about a child's ability.

Handwritten words, free-form calculations and essays are a different problem. Handwriting capture requires a validated recognition workflow and is not a general-launch promise. Later essay assistance would remain teacher-reviewed, never an automatic final grade.

Downloaded resources, a local register and supported local marking are intended to remain usable with limited connectivity. New cloud-generated content, networked responses and synchronisation require a connection. The teacher needs clear saved/pending status so work is not mistaken for a successfully shared record.

## 5. Turn learning evidence into a next teaching step

A score by itself does not tell the teacher what to do next. The proposed teaching assistant would help the teacher evaluate recorded learning evidence, understand what needs attention and decide how to respond. It supports the teacher's understanding of a child; it does not claim to know the whole child from a score.

> **Concept illustration · proposed general launch**
>
> Observed in this fictional exercise: Aiman Hakim can identify equal parts but struggles when comparing two fractions.
>
> Suggested next step: revisit equal-sized wholes with a picture-based task.
>
> Teacher decision: use the suggestion, adapt it, or check understanding through a short conversation first.

At general launch, these suggestions would be based on explainable rules and recorded evidence. They are not a diagnosis or proof of mastery. A wrong answer may reflect wording, an unclear scan, distraction or a missing concept; the teacher interprets it in context.

The intended loop is **understand → support → follow up**, grounded in recorded evidence and teacher judgement. For an individual student, the teacher could review a difficulty, try a different explanation or a short personal conversation, then check another response. A generated exercise would serve that chosen action. New evidence helps the teacher review progress; it does not by itself prove what caused a change.

Formal **Tahap Penguasaan**, or mastery-level judgements, remain the teacher's responsibility. Attendance alone must not be treated as evidence of learning mastery.

For groups and the whole class, the proposed general-launch class view would surface shared difficulties through explainable rules. For example, a teacher might choose a small-group explanation when several students struggle with the same concept, or revisit it with the class. These are illustrative teacher choices, not automatic grouping or fixed ability labels. Class views, rule-based flags and suggestions are planned for all teachers at launch. Plain-language AI analysis, teacher assistant conversations and AI-written administrative drafts enter a guided trial with selected teachers at that same launch. Automatic history-based individualised material at scale remains Fast-follow. All require reliable records and validation; teachers choose the response.

## 6. Make progress understandable for students and parents

Family access should help learning, not become continuous surveillance. The proposed family surface would show selected information about the parent's own child, including curated progress, attendance and announcements. Summaries would be periodic and teacher-approved rather than a live stream of every mistake.

> **Concept illustration · proposed general launch**
>
> **What we practised:** comparing fractions with pictures.
>
> **What is becoming clearer:** identifying equal parts of a whole.
>
> **What still needs practice:** comparing fractions when the pictures look different.
>
> **Try together:** draw two equal-sized rectangles, shade half of one and a quarter of the other, and ask your child to explain the difference.
>
> Prepared for review by the teacher before sharing. Fictional example, not a real student record.

Younger students can benefit through classroom support and parent-guided activities without their own account. Age-appropriate student access is part of the proposed Core family experience; exact age bands and permissions still need to be settled. Older students should receive an appropriate view of their own learning, not unrestricted access to other records.

Parents and students would use their own suitable devices. No student device supply is included. The teacher's classroom and paper-based routes remain important where home access is limited.

Direct parent–teacher messaging is Fast-follow, not a launch claim. A full raw gradebook is not the starting family experience. The priority is a clear explanation of learning and a useful next action.

Where permitted, a later experience could let a teacher select a textbook chapter, prepare a discussion prompt and generate draft practice grounded in that content. The content's origin should remain understandable and the teacher would still check the result.

This depends on explicit rights and usable access for the intended processing, not merely the ability to open an e-book. The general-launch curriculum-led experience remains the starting route while those arrangements are resolved.

## 8. One complete vision, with a clear delivery journey

The product is proposed, not an already-deployed classroom application. The stages below explain intended availability; they do not assert completed development or fixed rollout dates.

| Stage | Intended experience | Release condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General launch: Core | Template-assisted lesson planning, AI practice, structured assessment, student summaries, class views with rule-based flags/suggestions, notice/reminder templates, classroom tools, shared resources and curated family access. | Teaching quality, supported device workflows, safeguarding, accessibility and operational readiness validated. |
| Guided beta at the same launch | Full AI-drafted lesson plans, plain-language AI analysis, teacher assistant conversations and AI-written administrative drafts for selected teachers, through shared underlying services. | Supervised use, documented quality review and feedback; broader access only after evaluation. |
| Fast-follow | Automatic history-based individualised material, direct messaging and dependency-bound content/capture features. | Relevant product quality, data, rights, permissions and support readiness established. |
| Later | More advanced assistance, such as teacher-reviewed essay support and carefully governed early-warning tools. | Separate validation of usefulness, safety and a real follow-up process. |

### What belongs in Core

All six pillars contribute to the proposed first release:

1. **Learning records:** formative checks, structured multiple-choice marking, a standalone attendance register and teacher-maintained mastery records.
2. **Classroom activity:** teacher-led polls/quizzes and offline access to supported local tasks and downloaded resources.
3. **Preparation:** curriculum-linked practice drafts, reading-level adaptation, lesson-plan templates and the same-launch full-plan beta.
4. **Teaching support:** student summaries, class views and rule-based flags with explainable follow-up suggestions.
5. **Everyday efficiency:** communication templates and reusable lesson-plan sharing.
6. **Family access:** curated progress, attendance, announcements and age-appropriate periodic visibility.

The proposed product requirement covers BM, English, Mandarin and Tamil, including onboarding and generated output. Each requires its own quality validation. The broader intended scope spans Standard 1 to Form 5; precise year, subject and language readiness must be mapped before rollout promises. This is not a claim that every curriculum combination is already complete.

For device planning, we recommend an M1-or-later iPad, with A16 as the proposed minimum supported model. The application must be validated on both target categories; core usefulness must not depend on every device having the same on-device AI capability. Final hardware and deployment arrangements remain with the relevant programme parties. Device prices are outside this document.

Accessibility is a Core acceptance requirement: readable text, suitable contrast, keyboard/input alternatives and assistive-technology testing. WCAG 2.2 AA is the target for the web experience, complemented by native iPad testing. This is a requirement to demonstrate, not a certification claim. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), checked 7 September 2026.

## 9. Help teachers start, and keep helping them

Introducing an application is not the same as making it useful in a busy school. The proposed onboarding should guide a teacher through real first tasks: record attendance, prepare an exercise, check a response and create a lesson plan.

Short in-app guidance, reusable training material and school-level champions would support that first-use experience. Teachers should be able to ask for help without becoming software specialists.

| Proposed responsibility | Lead proposed for discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Product onboarding materials and trainer guidance | Sifututor / Learnest Lab |
| Platform issues, technical escalation and fixes | Sifututor / Learnest Lab |
| School participation and training arrangements | Kota Buku |
| First-line enquiries and routing unresolved issues | Kota Buku |
| Review of successful use and adoption difficulties | Joint review |

The help journey should be straightforward: **teacher asks → first-line support resolves or routes → technical team investigates where needed → teacher receives an answer → recurring issues inform improvement**.

This division is our proposed operating arrangement. Staffing capacity, training schedules, response times and service levels still require agreement. Device, connectivity and distribution delivery are outside our software commitment and need their own named support routes. No outside training body's delivery capacity is assumed.

Joint reviews should ask whether teachers can complete useful tasks, whether material needs excessive correction and whether support problems recur. Account creation and login counts alone would not establish adoption. Whether adoption is mandatory or optional remains for Kota Buku and the relevant education authorities to determine.

## 10. Keep teachers in control and information protected

The proposed design uses learning information to support teaching and suitable reporting. It does not require collecting every available fact about a student or using teacher activity as a performance-ranking tool.

| Information | Proposed access boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Student learning records | Relevant teachers see their own students; school leadership access is tied to a documented duty. |
| Parent or student view | Their own permitted, curated information with age-appropriate controls. |
| Individual teacher workflow information | The teacher and immediate school leader only for reporting; no above-school individual teacher drill-down. |
| Wider education reporting | Aggregated views; any individual student access requires a separately authorised purpose and logged process. This does not widen access to individual teacher workflow information. |

Teacher workflow information must not feed appraisal, disciplinary action or individual rankings. These restrictions belong in contracts as well as access controls. Public school or class league tables are not part of the proposed offer.

Identifiable information must remain in Malaysia throughout processing, including AI processing, logs and backups. That requirement has to be verified for the actual services selected; a provider's local presence alone is insufficient. Exceptional service or security access must be limited, logged and reviewed.

The delivery arrangements must identify who controls the data and who processes it on their instructions, the reasons for collection, suitable parent/student notices, retention rules, correction/export routes and incident responsibilities. Those legal and operational schedules are not settled by this proposal. No sale of student data or independent vendor reuse is proposed.

If a record is wrong or a family has a concern, there must be an understandable way to raise it and a named route for response. Trust is part of the intended daily experience, not a legal document hidden behind it.

## 11. Relevant experience, followed by product-specific proof

Sifututor brings education-platform experience; Learnest Lab provides the visible product technology. Our internal review dated 6 September 2026 identified relevant patterns in attendance handling, controlled parent views, teacher-led learning services and checks around AI-generated outputs.

That experience informs this proposal. It does not mean an existing product can simply be renamed as the Kota Buku classroom app. The native iPad experience, offline behaviour, school/curriculum model and content-grounding workflows require new development and validation for this use case.

### What the research supports

A December 2024 EEF/NFER trial in secondary-school science in England found lower preparation time with guided ChatGPT use, with no detected difference in sampled resource quality. It supports investigating this approach, not promising the same result for Malaysian teachers or this proposed product. [Evaluation report, executive summary](https://d2tic4wvo1iusb.cloudfront.net/production/documents/projects/chatgpt_in_lesson_planning_-_evaluation_report.pdf), checked 7 September 2026.

### What we would demonstrate here

| Evaluation question | Proposed evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Can a teacher finish the task more easily? | Compare the complete task before and during use, including corrections, setup and remaining official entry. |
| Are generated resources suitable? | Teacher review of correctness, curriculum fit, language and difficulty before broader exposure. |
| Does captured information support a sensible next action? | Check capture quality, traceability and the teacher's interpretation of follow-up suggestions. |
| Does use continue after introduction? | Completed workflows, repeat use, support needs and feedback across participating contexts. |
| Do students and parents understand the information? | Appropriate comprehension checks and feedback, not just portal visits. |

Success criteria and review responsibilities should be agreed before evaluation. Learning gains require their own suitable evaluation; they cannot be inferred from faster planning or more frequent logins. The offer is a concrete product direction with a path to evidence, not a guaranteed outcome.

Mulakan dengan bab 1–6 untuk memahami pengalaman guru, murid dan ibu bapa. Bab 7–10 menerangkan peluang Kota Buku, pelaksanaan dan perlindungan. Bab 11–12 merangkumi pengalaman berkaitan, penilaian dan perbincangan komersial seterusnya. Indeks ciri dan glosari menyediakan rujukan ringkas.

## 1. Pengalaman yang bersambung, berpusatkan pengajaran

Kerja guru tidak berakhir apabila rancangan pengajaran siap. Pelajaran memerlukan latihan yang sesuai, jawapan perlu disemak, dan kesukaran murid perlu membantu guru merancang pelajaran seterusnya. Ibu bapa juga memerlukan penerangan kemajuan yang mudah difahami.

Kami mencadangkan aplikasi bilik darjah Kota Buku yang menghubungkan tugasan ini. Guru akan menggunakan ruang kerja iPad untuk membuat persediaan, mengajar, meneliti bukti pembelajaran dan memilih sokongan susulan. Pengalaman web berasingan yang ringkas akan memberi ibu bapa dan murid yang sesuai mengikut umur akses kepada maklumat yang dibenarkan melalui peranti sendiri.

Matlamatnya praktikal dan bercita-cita tinggi: memberi guru lebih ruang untuk perhatian peribadi, bukan sekadar menjana bahan lebih pantas. Pembantu pengajaran yang dicadangkan akan membantu guru **memahami setiap murid dan kelas → memilih sokongan sesuai → meneliti kemajuan susulan**. Bukti direkodkan dan peraturan yang boleh diterangkan menjadi asas; bantuan AI lebih mendalam dibina secara berperingkat seperti dalam bab 5. Menjana latihan ialah cara menyokong keputusan pengajaran, bukan keseluruhan tujuan. Sentuhan peribadi dan pertimbangan pentaksiran rasmi kekal pada guru.

| Pengguna | Perkara yang ingin dimudahkan |
| --- | --- |
| Guru | Mengguna semula struktur pelajaran, menyediakan latihan sesuai, menyemak jawapan berstruktur dan memilih pengajaran seterusnya daripada rekod yang bersambung. |
| Murid | Menerima sokongan kelas yang lebih jelas dan latihan sesuai, termasuk aktiviti berasaskan kertas tanpa peranti peribadi. |
| Ibu bapa | Menerima penerangan kemajuan yang diluluskan guru dan cara mudah menyokong pembelajaran di rumah. |
| Kota Buku | Menggabungkan kandungan, penyelarasan program dan penggunaan bilik darjah dalam cadangan yang menyeluruh, bersama pelaksanaan produk dan teknologi Sifututor. |

Ini ialah pengalaman enam bahagian yang bermakna, bukan sekumpulan arahan AI yang terpisah. Ia menghubungkan rekod pembelajaran murid, aktiviti kelas, penyediaan kandungan, sokongan pengajaran, kecekapan harian dan akses keluarga. Bab 8 membezakan skop pelancaran yang dicadangkan daripada peluasan seterusnya.

## 2. Apakah yang berubah dalam hari seorang guru?

Soalan yang berguna bukan sekadar “Bolehkah AI menulis ini?” Sebaliknya, “Bahagian manakah boleh dipermudahkan sambil mengekalkan pertimbangan guru?”

Aliran berikut ialah ilustrasi, bukan dapatan bahawa setiap sekolah bekerja dengan cara yang sama atau dakwaan penjimatan masa yang telah diukur.

| Tugasan | Penambahbaikan yang dicadangkan | Perkara yang masih dilakukan guru |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menyediakan pelajaran | Bermula dengan struktur boleh guna semula dan objektif kurikulum yang dipilih. | Menentukan tujuan, urutan dan kesesuaian pelajaran. |
| Menyediakan latihan berbeza | Meminta draf soalan atau penerangan lebih mudah berdasarkan objektif. | Menyemak ketepatan, bahasa dan tahap kesukaran sebelum digunakan. |
| Menyemak jawapan berstruktur | Merakam helaian aneka pilihan yang disokong dan menyemak berdasarkan skema jawapan. | Membetulkan rakaman yang tidak jelas dan mentafsir makna jawapan. |
| Merancang susulan | Meneliti kekuatan dan kesukaran murid yang direkodkan bersama cadangan yang boleh dijejaki. | Memilih sama ada mengajar semula, menukar latihan atau mengumpul bukti tambahan. |
| Memaklumkan ibu bapa | Menyesuaikan templat komunikasi dan berkongsi ringkasan kemajuan terpilih. | Menentukan maklumat yang sesuai dikongsi dan meluluskannya. |

Pengurangan yang disasarkan ialah penyediaan draf berulang, pengumpulan maklumat secara manual dan penyediaan maklumat serupa di beberapa tempat. Ia bukan janji menghapuskan semua tugasan pentadbiran. Fasa pertama akan beroperasi secara berasingan daripada sistem Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia. Eksport umum boleh membantu semakan dan pengurusan rekod, tetapi kemasukan rasmi yang diwajibkan mungkin masih perlu dibuat secara berasingan.

Kami akan mengukur keseluruhan tugasan, termasuk pembetulan AI, semakan rakaman dan kemasukan berulang yang masih diperlukan. Draf yang lebih pantas hanya berguna jika bahan akhirnya sesuai dan keseluruhan aliran benar-benar membantu guru.

## 3. Sediakan pelajaran dan latihan yang sesuai

Bayangkan Cikgu Farah menyediakan pelajaran pecahan untuk 4 Bestari. Semua nama dan rekod dalam contoh ini ialah rekaan. Contoh menerangkan interaksi, bukan pemetaan kurikulum yang diperakui, templat rasmi rancangan pengajaran atau tangkap layar aplikasi yang sudah beroperasi.

### Bermula dengan perkara yang perlu difahami murid

Guru memilih mata pelajaran, tahun dan objektif pembelajaran berkaitan. Aplikasi akan menyusun pilihan kurikulum menggunakan **DSKP**, iaitu Dokumen Standard Kurikulum dan Pentaksiran yang berkenaan. Guru kemudian boleh melengkapkan **RPH**, atau Rancangan Pengajaran Harian, yang berstruktur tanpa membina susun atur semula setiap kali.

> **Ilustrasi konsep · cadangan pelancaran umum**
>
> Fokus pelajaran: membandingkan pecahan mudah menggunakan keseluruhan yang sama saiz.
>
> Pembukaan: minta murid membandingkan dua gambar dengan keseluruhan yang sama tetapi pembahagian berbeza.
>
> Permintaan latihan: sediakan draf latihan aneka pilihan ringkas dan versi dengan lebih banyak bantuan bergambar.
>
> Semakan guru: sahkan gambar, skema jawapan, bahasa dan kesesuaian sebelum dicetak.

Aliran yang dicadangkan ialah: **pilih objektif → gunakan templat → minta latihan → semak dan sesuaikan → mengajar**. Pustaka rancangan pengajaran bersama membolehkan guru mengguna semula struktur yang sesuai dan menambah baiknya dari semasa ke semasa.

### Berikan draf berguna, bukan jawapan muktamad

Latihan janaan AI dan penyesuaian aras bacaan termasuk dalam cadangan pelancaran umum. Guru menyemak sama ada soalan mempunyai jawapan yang sah, penerangannya tepat dan bahasanya sesuai dengan kelas. Bahan tidak sesuai boleh disunting, ditolak atau diganti dengan sumber sedia ada yang telah diluluskan guru.

Rancangan pengajaran penuh janaan AI juga akan dibangunkan untuk pelancaran yang sama, tetapi akses awal melalui **beta berpandu untuk guru terpilih**. Ini bermaksud penggunaan awal dengan bimbingan dan maklum balas kualiti sebelum diperluas. Ia bukan pembangunan berasingan pada masa kemudian, dan bukan janji bahawa semua guru menerima penjanaan rancangan penuh serta-merta.

Pendekatan rujukan awal menggunakan dokumen kurikulum awam, tanpa bergantung pada pemprosesan buku teks Kota Buku. Terma penggunaan sumber yang tepat masih perlu disahkan. Penyediaan draf berasaskan buku teks hanya menyusul setelah hak dan akses yang boleh digunakan diperoleh. Pengesahan bahan janaan oleh guru diperlukan sebelum pelancaran.

## 4. Mengajar, mengumpul respons dan menyemak kefahaman

iPad menyokong pelajaran tanpa mewajibkan setiap murid memiliki peranti. Guru boleh memaparkan aktiviti melalui peralatan paparan kelas yang tersedia, menggunakan latihan bercetak atau membenarkan murid yang layak menjawab melalui peranti sendiri apabila akses dan sambungan mengizinkan.

Kaedah ini menghasilkan bukti yang berbeza:

| Kaedah di kelas | Perkara yang boleh disokong | Batasan penting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perbincangan dikendalikan guru atau soalan dipaparkan | Penerangan dan perbincangan bersama. | Penyertaan sahaja tidak mewujudkan rekod jawapan dikenal pasti bagi setiap murid. |
| Helaian aneka pilihan dengan bulatan/tanda yang disokong | Merakam respons berstruktur dan menyemak berdasarkan skema jawapan. | Guru perlu menyelesaikan tanda yang kabur dan mengesahkan padanan murid dengan helaian. |
| Kuiz dalam aplikasi melalui peranti murid yang tersedia | Respons yang boleh dipadankan apabila identiti murid dikenal pasti dengan sewajarnya. | Memerlukan akses, peranti dan sambungan sesuai; peranti tidak dibekalkan kepada murid melalui cadangan ini. |

Untuk kaedah kertas, urutannya ialah **rakam → semak tanda kabur → sahkan identiti → semak jawapan → simpan bukti pembelajaran**. Ini ialah pemarkahan berstruktur, bukan pertimbangan AI tentang kebolehan kanak-kanak.

Perkataan bertulis tangan, jalan kira dan esei ialah masalah berbeza. Rakaman tulisan tangan memerlukan proses pengecaman yang disahkan dan bukan janji pelancaran umum. Bantuan esei pada peringkat kemudian tetap memerlukan semakan guru, bukan gred akhir automatik.

Sumber yang dimuat turun, daftar setempat dan pemarkahan setempat yang disokong disasarkan kekal boleh digunakan ketika sambungan terhad. Kandungan awan baharu, respons melalui rangkaian dan penyegerakan memerlukan sambungan. Guru memerlukan status simpanan/tertangguh yang jelas supaya kerja tidak disalah anggap telah berjaya dikongsi.

## 5. Jadikan bukti pembelajaran langkah pengajaran seterusnya

Markah sahaja tidak memberitahu guru tindakan seterusnya. Pembantu pengajaran yang dicadangkan akan membantu guru menilai bukti pembelajaran direkodkan, memahami perkara yang memerlukan perhatian dan memilih tindakan. Ia menyokong pemahaman guru tentang murid; bukan mendakwa mengenali keseluruhan diri murid daripada markah.

> **Ilustrasi konsep · cadangan pelancaran umum**
>
> Pemerhatian dalam latihan rekaan ini: Aiman Hakim mengenal pasti bahagian sama besar tetapi menghadapi kesukaran membandingkan dua pecahan.
>
> Cadangan susulan: ulang konsep keseluruhan sama saiz melalui tugasan bergambar.
>
> Keputusan guru: gunakan cadangan, sesuaikannya atau semak kefahaman melalui perbualan ringkas terlebih dahulu.

Pada pelancaran umum, cadangan ini berasaskan peraturan yang boleh diterangkan dan bukti yang direkodkan. Ia bukan diagnosis atau bukti penguasaan muktamad. Jawapan salah mungkin berpunca daripada bahasa soalan, imbasan tidak jelas, gangguan perhatian atau konsep yang belum difahami; guru mentafsirkannya mengikut konteks.

Kitaran yang dicadangkan ialah **fahami → bantu → susuli**, berasaskan bukti direkodkan dan pertimbangan guru. Bagi murid individu, guru boleh meneliti kesukaran, mencuba penerangan berbeza atau perbualan peribadi ringkas, kemudian menyemak respons baharu. Latihan dijana menyokong tindakan pilihan guru itu. Bukti baharu membantu guru meneliti kemajuan; ia sendiri tidak membuktikan punca perubahan.

Pertimbangan **Tahap Penguasaan** rasmi kekal tanggungjawab guru. Kehadiran sahaja tidak boleh dianggap sebagai bukti penguasaan pembelajaran.

Bagi kumpulan dan seluruh kelas, paparan kelas yang dicadangkan untuk pelancaran umum membantu mengenal pasti kesukaran bersama melalui peraturan yang boleh diterangkan. Contohnya, guru boleh memilih penerangan kumpulan kecil apabila beberapa murid bergelut dengan konsep sama, atau mengulanginya bersama kelas. Ini ilustrasi pilihan guru, bukan pengelompokan automatik atau label kebolehan tetap. Paparan kelas, penanda dan cadangan berasaskan peraturan dirancang untuk semua guru ketika pelancaran. Analisis AI dalam bahasa mudah, perbualan pembantu AI untuk guru dan draf pentadbiran janaan AI melalui percubaan berpandu bersama guru terpilih pada pelancaran sama. Bahan individu automatik berdasarkan sejarah pembelajaran pada skala besar kekal Susulan awal. Semua ini memerlukan rekod boleh dipercayai dan pengesahan; guru memilih tindakan.

- Tanggungjawab yang dicadangkan, termasuk latihan, sokongan pertama, sokongan teknikal dan batasan berkaitan peranti.
- Pencapaian pelaksanaan, bukti penerimaan dan cara mengendalikan kebergantungan yang belum dipenuhi.
- Urusan perkhidmatan, harga, terma bayaran dan kawalan perubahan.
- Tanggungjawab kandungan, data, pengedaran dan kontrak yang perlu dipersetujui sebelum pelaksanaan.

Angka komersial akan diberikan melalui tawaran berasingan yang telah diselaraskan. Dokumen ini bukan sebut harga dan tidak membuktikan anugerah pembiayaan, kelulusan perolehan atau kerjasama yang ditandatangani.

Tujuan segera ialah supaya Kota Buku memahami nilai dan mempertimbangkan untuk membawa susunan program yang dicadangkan kepada perbincangan komersial berskop. Sifututor akan membawa cadangan produk serta logik pelaksanaannya; perbincangan kemudian boleh menumpukan pada susunan yang diperlukan untuk menjayakannya.
