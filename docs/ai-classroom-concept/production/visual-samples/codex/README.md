## Sample-specific copy

- Title: Satu matlamat pelajaran. Latihan yang sesuai. / One lesson goal. Practice that fits.
- Explanation: Pilih matlamat. Kecerdasan buatan membantu menyediakan pilihan latihan; guru menyemak dan memilih. / Choose a goal. Artificial intelligence helps draft practice options; the teacher reviews and chooses.
- Goal: Membandingkan pecahan; tentukan pecahan yang lebih besar. / Comparing fractions; identify the larger fraction.
- Template activity prompts: Terangkan, Berlatih, Bincangkan. / Explain, Practise, Discuss.
- Teacher controls: Guru boleh ubah; Semak latihan. / Teacher can edit; Review practice.
- Visual support: compare 1/2 and 1/4 with equal-length bars. Extension: compare 3/4 and 2/3 and explain. Both are synthetic, original examples, not certified curriculum material.
- Benefit: Kurangkan kerja berulang. Guru kekal membuat keputusan. / Less repeated preparation. Teaching decisions stay with the teacher.
- Delivery: Dirancang ketika pelancaran: rangka pelan dan bantuan latihan untuk semua guru; pelan penuh oleh kecerdasan buatan dalam percubaan berpandu untuk guru terpilih. / Planned at launch: templates and assisted practice for all teachers; full artificial-intelligence-drafted plans in a guided trial for selected teachers.
