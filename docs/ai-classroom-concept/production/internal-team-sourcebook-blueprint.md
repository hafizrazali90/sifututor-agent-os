## 1. Document contract

**Working title:** Kota Buku AI classroom programme: the complete team sourcebook.

**Purpose:** let a new team member understand the opportunity, research, product, decisions and delivery reasoning as if they had participated in the work. The reader should be able to explain the proposal, find the evidence, identify their responsibilities and distinguish settled choices from unresolved checks.

**Audience:** leadership, partner/commercial coordination, product, design, curriculum/pedagogy, engineering, QA, delivery/support, governance and future document/deck authors. Plain explanations serve the whole team; role-specific depth is available without making everyone read technical appendices first.

**Scope:** a complete synthesis of the existing research/specification and reconciled partner work, not a new product specification, expanded research programme, contract, software build plan authorised for execution, or dump of all private source material. The four specifications retain detailed authority and the partner owns figures. Latest approved decisions govern the synthesis; do not let copied historical recommendations override them.

**Editorial system:** each substantive chapter follows “in plain language → why it matters → how it works → alternatives and decision → operational/technical depth → evidence and limits → what this enables”. Make implications explicit instead of supplying unexplained links. Use prose plus small useful diagrams, matrices and examples. No arbitrary defect count, completion percentage or fixed page limit.

**Outputs after drafting:** governed BM/English Markdown, navigable offline HTML with one language at a time, separate single-language A4 PDF/DOCX editions. Product four-language coverage remains a requirement discussed here, not four document editions. No assets or exports produced in this blueprint packet.

## 3. Appendices and coverage requirements

- **A. Feature and workflow index:** every row in P §§3-8 mapped to I05 and relevant journey; no silent loss when summarising the MVP list. Cross-cutting onboarding, accessibility, languages and offline behavior included.
- **B. Field/event and authority dictionary:** index D §§5-8, explain how to read each field and purpose, link detailed spec. Do not duplicate sensitive real data.
- **C. Evidence and claim register:** original sources, dates, exact permitted claims, limitations and chapter uses; public statement versus measured result clearly separated. Preserve existing proposition and decision IDs.
- **D. Decision and assumption history:** full rationale, alternatives, owner approval, counterpart acceptance and verification as distinct columns; link REC and D/G records rather than create competing numbers.
- **E. Evaluation and acceptance catalogue:** proposed measurement definitions, representative teacher tasks, per-language/accessibility/capture checks and release evidence. Unknown thresholds remain unagreed, not zero or invented targets.
- **F. Glossary:** BM/English plain terms, including RPH, DSKP, TP, grounding, deterministic checks, processor/controller and proposed-versus-live status.
- **G. Source provenance and safe-reading index:** what each source is, access restrictions, dates and which review corrects which earlier statement; partner founder briefing is not a send-ready commercial document.

Completeness means a reader can reconstruct the reasoning and find the governing detail, not that every raw source must be pasted into the book. Key reasoning belongs in prose; links support it rather than replace explanation.

## 5. Evidence, research and sensitive boundaries

Use the shared source/claim schema; distinguish established facts, dated history, public statements, owner-approved strategy, proposals, interpretations and illustrations. Record factual gaps without rewriting settled choices. Known PR overstatements must not re-enter prose: use H §13/T §15's qualified legal treatment and primary references, with final applicability checks still open.

This blueprint reuses the completed research and the framework review. Before drafted claims are accepted, recover their original evidence and reverify selected volatile/high-impact facts within the existing scope. A new research question that materially changes scope goes to Hafiz. External/public claims must pass a stricter source and disclosure check than internal historical reasoning.

Do not include credentials, production/customer records, access methods, exploitable topology, raw private audits, legal advice or negotiation positions beyond the team's authorised need. Record a restricted issue's practical dependency and owner with a controlled-source pointer instead of copying its sensitive detail. Commercial figures stay in PF under its owner, not in this sourcebook or its appendices.

## 7. Acceptance and approval

Blueprint acceptance: chapter and appendix coverage, evidence pointers, roles, visual ownership, known gaps and production boundaries are explicit. Future full-document acceptance requires:

- A new team member can explain the proposition, full connected journey, why the main choices were made, readiness and unresolved checks without rereading all source files.
- Every six-pillar feature row and cross-cutting requirement has a chapter/index destination; all D1-D10/C4 and REC approvals map without contradiction.
- Full-RPH beta is alongside launch, not deferred; teacher-only supplied devices do not remove parent/pupil benefits; launch is not reduced to two tools.
- Research limitations, alternative choices and consequences if assumptions fail are explained, not merely linked.
- Responsibility, agreement and verification are separate; no invented Kota Buku staffing, school cohort, timeline, source right or contract commitment.
- Factual claims have dated original evidence, audiences and final-check status; commercial figures and sensitive source payload remain excluded.
- Generated editions pass content completeness, links, numbering, language parity, freshness, safe-payload and visual/print checks; independent review resolves blocking/material findings before local acceptance.
