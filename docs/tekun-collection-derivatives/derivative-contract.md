# TEKUN Collection Partnership — Derivative Package Contract

## Authority

- **Single governed source:** `docs/tekun-collection-partnership-sourcebook.md`, version 1.0, reviewed 31 August 2026.
- **Research authority:** `docs/tekun-collection-research/claim-register.md` and `source-register.md`.
- **This contract does not create new capability claims.** It controls selection, simplification and disclosure for three derived outputs.

## Shared Narrative

TEKUN Corporation is an established collection operator. The opportunity is to combine its relationships, authority and operating knowledge with a TEKUN Corporation-owned, governed digital platform and an authorised managed operating service. Value should be proved through discovery, a bounded pilot, reconciled evidence and customer-treatment guardrails before scaling.

## Deliverable 1 — Executive Stakeholder Presentation

- **Primary audience:** TEKUN Corporation leadership, board-level readers and selected decision-makers.
- **Purpose:** Enable an informed decision to authorise governed discovery and pilot co-design.
- **Length:** Approximately 16–20 slides.
- **Reader experience:** Business meaning first; one idea per slide; diagrams and decision logic over dense specification.
- **Include:** Context, opportunity, proposed partnership, ownership, customer-treatment principles, platform and service model, value pathway, capability evidence, pilot, milestones, H0–H5 roadmap and next decision.
- **Exclude:** Internal claim IDs on slide faces, detailed provider questions, security topology, full regulatory analysis, negotiation strategy, pricing or unverified results.
- **Outputs:** Editable Markdown slide script, standalone HTML deck and landscape PDF.

## Deliverable 2 — Controlled External Proposal

- **Primary audience:** TEKUN Corporation leadership, procurement, finance, compliance/legal/privacy, technology/data and authorised proposal reviewers.
- **Purpose:** Explain the proposed partnership, scope, governance, operating model, evidence plan and roadmap with enough detail for structured discussion.
- **Length:** Approximately 20–30 A4 pages.
- **Reader experience:** Executive summary followed by business, operating, technical and assurance depth.
- **Include:** Partnership proposition, roles, lifecycle, architecture, managed service, customer treatment, data/security, measurement, pilot, roadmap, transition readiness, discovery agenda and evidence notes.
- **Exclude:** Price, guaranteed improvement, final legal applicability, named undisclosed providers, private portfolio facts, confidential internal system topology or unsupported certification claims.
- **Outputs:** Governed Markdown, standalone HTML, A4 PDF and editable DOCX.

## Deliverable 3 — Internal Technical And Operating Presentation

- **Primary audience:** Internal product, engineering, operations, collection, finance/reconciliation, compliance/privacy, management and presentation creators.
- **Purpose:** Give internal teams enough shared knowledge to explain, design and prepare discovery without rereading the full sourcebook.
- **Length:** 35-slide core briefing plus a 7-slide optional working-reference appendix.
- **Reader experience:** Plain-language framing plus deeper architecture, controls, operating flows, ownership and open questions.
- **Include:** Full system model, record hierarchy, lifecycle controls, agent/supervisor workspace, money flow caveat, integrations, data/security, service management, team model, KPI dictionary, pilot, roadmap, risks, transition and discovery checklist.
- **Exclude:** Customer-identifiable data, credentials, exploitable topology, invented provider/commercial information and unapproved legal conclusions.
- **Outputs:** Editable Markdown slide script, standalone HTML deck and landscape PDF.

## Visual Direction

- Premium corporate editorial foundation.
- Deep navy and teal palette with warm sand accent.
- Clear hierarchy, generous whitespace and restrained technology character.
- Diagrams explain relationships; decorative visuals do not replace evidence.
- External outputs use simpler language and fewer internal control details.

## Shared Acceptance Rules

- Every material statement must be traceable to the accepted sourcebook or research pack.
- Proposed design must not be written as a current TEKUN capability.
- No price, guaranteed recovery, profit promise or target percentage.
- No portfolio-specific legal conclusion or claim that TCorp is registered with SKP.
- TEKUN ownership, customer-treatment guardrails and transition readiness remain explicit.
- All outputs must disclose that final scope depends on discovery and authorised decision gates.
- Derived formats are regenerated from their Markdown source after material edits.

## Approved Local Stop Point

All three derivative packages drafted, rendered and reviewed locally. Stop before commit, upload, publication, distribution, TEKUN contact, pricing or legal/commercial commitment.
