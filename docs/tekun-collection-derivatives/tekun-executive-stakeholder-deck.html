TEKUN CorporationIntegrated Collection Growth Partnership

A complete collection operating loop

Intake and validate → Segment → Assign → Notify → Verify → Engage → Outcome branch: promise, protect, payment route or approved escalation → Reconcile → Cease/close → Improve

Every stage has:

  • a named owner;
  • required information;
  • an allowed next action;
  • customer-treatment and authority controls;
  • an auditable event and outcome.

Protected branches handle disputes, hardship, vulnerability, complaints and legal holds without losing the core record.

Customer treatment is part of the operating system

  • Verify identity before discussing an account.
  • Enforce approved contact windows, frequency and channel rules.
  • Use controlled notices, scripts and evidence.
  • Stop or redirect activity for settlement, dispute, hardship, vulnerability or legal hold.
  • Keep complaints accessible and traceable.
  • Representatives never receive customer money.
  • Preserve human authority for consequential outcomes.

Final controls must follow confirmed portfolio applicability and TEKUN-approved policy.

Prove value safely through a bounded pilot

A fair pilot needs

  • one principal or one defined cohort;
  • a written baseline and comparable history;
  • agreed outcomes and customer-treatment guardrails;
  • fixed measurement definitions and exclusions;
  • weekly operating review and monthly governance review;
  • explicit expand, adjust, re-baseline or stop decisions.
