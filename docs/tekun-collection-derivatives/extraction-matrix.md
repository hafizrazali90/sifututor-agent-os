# TEKUN Derivative Extraction And Disclosure Matrix

## Audience Key

- **E:** Executive stakeholder presentation.
- **P:** Controlled external proposal.
- **I:** Internal technical and operating presentation.
- **Summary:** Simplify while preserving meaning.
- **Detail:** Use the sourcebook's controlled depth.
- **Discovery only:** State the question or dependency; do not imply an answer.

## Never Extract Without New Owner Approval

- Price, fee model, revenue share, success fee or cost schedule.
- Recovery, collection, profit, savings or revenue targets.
- Active portfolio values, customer/account numbers or arrears profile.
- Current provider names, contracts, commissions or transition terms.
- Final regulatory applicability, registration, declaration or legal-status conclusions.
- Final data-controller/processor or DPO conclusions.
- Launch dates, staffing commitments, service levels or certifications.

## External Extraction Check

Before the executive deck or proposal is shared:

1. confirm the recipient and purpose;
2. reverify volatile public and regulatory claims;
3. remove internal-only notes and claim identifiers from slide faces;
4. ensure proposed design remains future tense;
5. verify no private provider, portfolio, pricing or legal conclusion appears;
6. confirm TEKUN ownership, transition readiness and customer-treatment safeguards remain clear;
7. obtain owner approval for the exact exported file.
