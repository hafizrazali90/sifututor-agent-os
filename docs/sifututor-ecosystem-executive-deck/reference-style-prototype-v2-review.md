# Sifututor Ecosystem Reference-Style Prototype V2 — Review Note

## Why V2 exists

The first reference-style prototype reproduced the supplied deck's palette,
typography and rounded-card structure but remained visibly too sparse. V2
adopts the reference's more important behaviour: purposeful visual density.

## Density rule now applied

Each main composition uses:

- one dominant visual;
- one supporting visual or evidence fragment;
- three to five meaningful annotations;
- one evidence or status layer;
- restrained decorative punctuation.

No invented metric or decorative fake interface was added to fill space.

## Material improvements

1. **Hero collage:** added tutor-fit, delivery and support annotations, an
   operations portrait and a concise operating-proof rail.
2. **Operating context:** moved both photographs upward, removed empty card
   space and added responsibility, continuity and shared-context overlays.
3. **Connected solution:** added actual Creative Hub and labelled Learnest
   prototype evidence, plus compact capability-detail chips.
4. **Milestones and expansion:** added milestone evidence tags, reusable
   foundation modules, country-localisation factors and service-vertical
   workflow factors.

## Checks completed

- Four slides rendered at 1920 × 1080.
- Every slide visually inspected individually and as a four-slide montage.
- Hero checked at 1280 × 720 and 500 × 900 uniform scaling.
- All local images and SVG assets resolved.
- Keyboard and button navigation passed.
- Reduced-motion behavior is present.
- No clipping, unintended overlap, broken image, 3D or isometric treatment was
  found in the final inspected render.

## Boundary

This is a local design-review prototype. It is not committed, uploaded,
published or externally shared. Full-deck production remains a separate next
step after owner acceptance of V2.
