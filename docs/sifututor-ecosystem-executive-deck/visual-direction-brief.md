## Governing visual idea

The presentation should feel like a contemporary creative-technology case study: highly designed, energetic and confident, but grounded in real product proof.

The visual language is inspired by the supplied reference without copying its brand, copy, exact layouts or proprietary identity. Sifututor's own systems, colours, milestones, logos and operating story remain authoritative.

## Design system

### Colour

- Deep indigo/purple as the principal brand field.
- Sifututor orange for decisive emphasis.
- Electric lime as a selective editorial highlighter.
- Cyan as the technology and connection accent.
- Near-black and warm white for contrast and reading surfaces.

Lime and orange are emphasis colours, not default backgrounds for every element.

### Typography

- Oversized uppercase editorial headlines.
- Tight line spacing and strong weight contrast.
- Important phrases may sit on flat angled highlight strips.
- Short supporting copy only; detailed explanation belongs in later reading-first slides or notes.
- Large dates and numbers carry milestones and sequence.

### Composition

- Asymmetric modular grids.
- Full-width colour blocks and deliberate white space.
- Flat screenshot panels with small rotations, hard crops and simple shadows.
- Graphic labels attached like editorial stickers.
- Flat starbursts, arrows, underlines and print-texture accents used sparingly.
- No perspective device rendering or pseudo-3D environments.

## Slide 1 — Cover and capability statement

### Message

Small team. Complete ecosystem.

### Composition

- Deep indigo editorial canvas.
- Official Sifututor logo on a light plaque.
- Oversized title with `Complete ecosystem` highlighted in lime.
- Flat collage of actual Ripple, Creative Hub and Learnest interface evidence.
- Compact 2018 to May 2025 milestone treatment.
- Orange and lime flat ornaments only.

## Slide 2 — Actual platform portfolio

### Message

One service core. Many specialised systems.

### Composition

- Warm-white magazine canvas with strong black title treatment.
- SIMS represented as the central authoritative operating core.
- Ripple and Creative Hub receive the largest real-interface panels.
- Parent App, Tutor App, Finch, Learnest, Kelasapp and Agent OS appear as specialised capability tiles.
- Product state remains explicit; Learnest evidence is labelled `Prototype`.
- Layout behaves as an editorial mosaic, not an architecture diagram.

## Slide 3 — End-to-end operating loop

### Message

A learning need becomes a managed, measurable service.

### Composition

- Near-black field with lime headline strip and orange/cyan blocks.
- Five large numbered stages arranged as a dynamic editorial sequence rather than a swimlane.
- Stages: demand and request; discover and match; teach and verify; bill, collect and pay; support, learn and improve.
- Actual Ripple interface evidence supports operational and improvement stages.
- Flat arrows establish continuity and return without creating a conventional process diagram.

## Asset rules

- Actual product screenshots are the principal visual evidence.
- Every screenshot must pass relevance, legibility, truth and privacy checks.
- Logos retain original proportions and receive adequate contrast.
- Prototype and demo evidence is labelled visibly.
- Personal names, contact data, financial figures, operational metrics, temporary warnings and broken states are excluded.
- Decorative elements must never resemble additional systems or imply unsupported capability.

## Board acceptance criteria

1. No isometric or 3D visual appears anywhere in the active board.
2. Each slide reads within five seconds and has one dominant assertion.
3. Actual interface evidence is visually prominent and accurately labelled.
4. The layout feels specific to Sifututor rather than like a generic corporate template.
5. Text remains readable at 1920×1080 and under uniform smaller-viewport scaling.
6. No sensitive, volatile or misleading product content appears.
7. HTML navigation, local assets and the landscape PDF proof pass visual QA.
