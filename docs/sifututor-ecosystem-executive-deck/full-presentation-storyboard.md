## Evidence rules

- Current capability, verified history, prototype and strategic direction remain visually distinct.
- Sifututor and Nakngaji metrics or product truth are never combined.
- No confidential financial values, unverified scale metrics or launch dates appear.
- SIMS remains authoritative for the core tutoring operation.
- Ripple remains a specialised staff workspace, not a replacement database.
- Learnest evidence remains visibly labelled `Prototype`.
- Expansion readiness is not presented as a completed country or vertical launch.
