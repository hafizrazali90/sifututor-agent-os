# Codex Prototype — Composition Notes

## Slide 1 — Managed service

The human learning photograph is the dominant visual anchor. The service stages are placed directly inside that scene, while the actual Ripple interface and controlled-money/learning panels explain the operational machinery beside it. The slide deliberately avoids a detached swimlane.

## Slide 2 — System portfolio

SIMS forms a tall authoritative spine. Ripple receives the largest interface-led area, and the remaining capabilities are expressed through a varied mosaic containing real interface crops, a mobile scene, specialist products and operating enablement. The visual hierarchy reflects system roles rather than giving every system an equal card.

## Slide 3 — Capability roadmap

The three near-term capability horizons form compact evidence cards. The lower field is dominated by two visual directions: a product-reuse network and a country-localisation map. This distinguishes consolidation from multiplication and adaptation without presenting illustrative directions as committed launch dates.

## Shared choices

- Fixed 1920 × 1080 editorial canvas.
- Locally bundled Manrope font.
- Sifututor navy, blue, cyan, orange, peach and cream palette.
- Real Sifututor imagery and existing verified interface assets.
- No invented metrics, no 3D illustration and no external dependencies.
