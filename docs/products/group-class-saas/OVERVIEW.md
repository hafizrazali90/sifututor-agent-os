## The Problem

Running a group learning business in Malaysia today means juggling three or four broken tools at once.

A typical madrasah or tuition centre operator manages:

- **Students** in a spreadsheet or basic system
- **Attendance** via group chat or manual registers — compiled by hand at the end of the month
- **Fees** chased individually over WhatsApp with no payment tracking
- **Teacher pay** calculated manually from session logs that may or may not be accurate
- **Class health** — nobody actually knows until a student drops out without warning

The result: operators are doing administrative work instead of running their business. They can't see which classes are thriving, which students are at risk of leaving, or whether they collected enough this month to cover teacher pay.

> 79.8% of Malaysian students attend tuition classes. The average family spends RM464/month on education. Yet the platforms running these businesses haven't meaningfully improved in a decade.

## What Is Kelasapp?

**Kelasapp is a multi-tenant SaaS platform built for any operator running structured group learning.**

It manages the complete operational cycle of a group class business — from creating classes and enrolling students, to marking attendance, collecting fees, and paying teachers — with AI-powered insights layered throughout.

One platform. One login. Everything connected.

Kelas is designed for:
- Islamic learning centres (kelas mengaji, madrasah, tahfiz)
- Tuition centres (all subjects)
- Online academies (Google Meet / Zoom based)
- Physical learning centres
- Any operator running recurring group sessions

**Kelasapp is not** a 1-to-1 tutoring marketplace. It is not a school ERP. It is not a generic CRM. It is purpose-built for the group class model.

## Core Features

### 1 · Class Management

The group class is Kelas's core entity — not the student, not the centre. Every other feature is built around it.

- Create classes with type (small group, large group, individual, family, online, physical), capacity, schedule, and assigned teacher
- **Live class health signal** — every class is automatically rated green, yellow, or red based on enrolment and attendance
- Admin sees all classes at a glance on one screen — no clicking into each one
- Sessions are pre-generated from the schedule; cancelled sessions are excluded from pay and reporting

### 2 · Student Management

- Student profiles with contact details, level, and guardian information
- Enrol students into one or more classes with a single action
- Level tracking — what each student is studying, with full history
- Mid-cycle join support — record where a student entered (e.g., "started at Surah Al-Baqarah, ayat 45") so teachers always know the context
- Student status: active, paused, dropped, graduated — drives billing and attendance automatically

### 3 · Attendance

Attendance is the highest-frequency action in Kelas. It is built for speed.

- Teacher opens today's class on their phone → taps present/absent/late for each student → done in under 2 minutes
- "Mark all present" with individual corrections for fast classes
- **Admin cross-class dashboard** — all classes, all sessions, one screen. No navigation required.
- Automatic absence alerts — students with 3+ consecutive absences surface for follow-up
- Attendance rate feeds directly into class health (green / yellow / red)
- Reports export to PDF and CSV

### 4 · Billing & Invoicing

Fee collection without the chase.

- **Automatic monthly invoice generation** — invoices are created on the 25th for the following month, covering all of a student's active enrolments
- **Configurable burn policy** — choose whether absent students are billed in full (most common for kelas mengaji) or prorated
- **FPX payment link** — one click generates a link the operator can paste into WhatsApp; the guardian pays online
- Manual payment recording for cash and bank transfers
- **Non-payment queue** — overdue invoices in one list, with a pre-filled message template the operator can send in one click
- Partial payment support
- LHDN e-invoice compliance fields captured (submission tooling in Phase 2)

### 5 · Teacher Management

- Teacher profiles with pay rates per class type
- Session logging is automatic — when a teacher marks attendance, that session counts toward their pay
- **Automatic pay calculation** at month-end: sessions × rate per class type
- Pay slips accessible to teachers directly in their login
- Teacher grading (A/B/C) with history — tracks quality over time

### 6 · Operator Dashboard

The first screen after login answers: *how is my business right now?*

- Class health grid — every active class, colour-coded, at a glance
- Revenue summary — invoiced, collected, outstanding, collection rate
- Attendance rate across all classes
- Student count trend vs last month
- **Action queue** — overdue invoices, absence alerts, unmarked sessions, in priority order

### 7 · AI Layer

No competitor has real AI. Kelasapp is built AI-first from day one.

- **Class health narrative** — "Kelas Asas B has been below 50% attendance for 3 weeks. 4 students have unpaid invoices since June." Plain English, auto-generated.
- **Follow-up message drafting** — click once, get a ready-to-send WhatsApp message tailored to the student's history. Operator edits and sends.
- **At-risk detection** — students ranked by risk of dropping out, based on attendance and payment history
- **Placement recommendation** — input a new student's level; AI suggests the best matching class

## Why Kelasapp Wins

Every existing platform in the market leaves at least one critical gap. Kelas was designed around the five gaps nobody has solved.

### Gap 1 — The Group Class Is the Core Entity

Every competitor models the student or the centre as the primary object. Kelas models the **group class**. For kelas mengaji operators, the class is the unit of business — one teacher, one cohort, one recurring session, one rotating syllabus. Everything in Kelas is built around it.

### Gap 2 — One Platform for Admin + Curriculum Tracking

No Malaysian product today can show you: "Student X is on Surah Al-Baqarah ayat 45, paid this month, attended 3 out of 4 sessions, taught by Ustaz Y who is rated A." Mudeer covers admin. Skooldash covers Hafazan. Kelas covers both.

### Gap 3 — LHDN E-Invoice Compliance for Islamic Operators

SimTrain has LHDN compliance — but only for secular tuition centres. None of the Islamic education platforms (Mudeer, SMAP, Skooldash) have it. As LHDN rolls out mandatory e-invoicing requirements, this becomes a compliance issue, not a nice-to-have.

### Gap 4 — Real AI (Not a Buzzword)

ClassFlow mentions "AI tools" vaguely. No competitor has streaming AI insights, smart follow-up drafting, or placement recommendations. This is Kelas's clearest product differentiation and the hardest to replicate.

### Gap 5 — Teacher Mobile Experience

No existing product has a strong teacher-facing interface for one-tap group attendance marking, real-time progress updates, and pay slip access. Teachers are the most underserved users in this market.

## Technology & Security

Kelasapp is built on a modern, secure, cloud-native stack.

- **Multi-tenant by design** — each operator's data is fully isolated. No operator can ever see another's students, classes, or financial data.
- **Authentication** via Better Auth — self-hosted authentication where each organisation is a tenant, with assigned operator, staff, and teacher roles. All auth data lives in our own Postgres, so there is no per-user auth vendor cost.
- **Payments** — FPX via FIUU (licensed Malaysian payment gateway). Platform subscriptions via Stripe. No card data is ever stored by Kelas.
- **Data residency** — hosted on KVM8 infrastructure in the region.
- **Uptime** — production-grade deployment with monitoring and automated alerting.
- **Backup** — daily automated database backups with point-in-time recovery.
