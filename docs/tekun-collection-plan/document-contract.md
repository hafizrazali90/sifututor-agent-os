# Document Contract — TEKUN Corporation Integrated Collection Growth Partnership

**Working subtitle:** Platform, managed operations, governance and measurable growth capability.

**Contract status:** Approved by Hafiz.

**Date:** 31 August 2026.

## Purpose

- **Why this document exists:** Give TEKUN Corporation stakeholders and our internal team a complete, evidence-governed knowledge source from which they can understand, evaluate and independently create presentations about the proposed partnership.
- **What should change after it is read:** Readers should understand why our platform-operating experience is relevant, what the TEKUN-owned solution and managed service would contain, how responsibility remains governed, how success would be measured and how capability could expand safely over time.
- **Primary audience:** TEKUN Corporation leadership, collection management, operations, compliance, finance, technology/data, procurement and board-level reviewers.
- **Secondary audience:** Our leadership, product, engineering, operations, finance, compliance, proposal writers and presentation creators; relevant TEKUN Nasional or portfolio-owner reviewers if TEKUN Corporation authorises sharing.

## Governing Direction

- **Central story:** TEKUN Corporation is an established credit-management and recovery operator. The partnership would modernise, integrate, scale and continuously improve that business through a TEKUN Corporation-owned and governed platform, built and operated by our team together with an authorised managed collection service.
- **Proof hierarchy:**
  1. TEKUN Corporation's established collection role and growth context.
  2. Sifututor's proven ability to operate complex, multi-stakeholder, financially sensitive live platforms.
  3. The proposed end-to-end collection platform, operating model and regulatory controls.
  4. A bounded pilot and governed scorecard that produce TEKUN-specific proof.
  5. A roadmap that compounds value through more portfolios, integrations, operating knowledge and intelligence.
- **Tone:** Respectful, confident, collaborative, evidence-led and commercially ambitious; never patronising, alarmist or predatory.
- **Depth:** Dense sourcebook depth with a plain-language first layer, operational detail second and technical/regulatory depth third.
- **Reader paths:** Executive, operations, compliance/risk, finance/performance, technology/data, procurement/partnership and presentation-creator paths.

## Scope

- **Must include:**
  - TEKUN Corporation profile, milestones and publicly evidenced collection context;
  - the opportunity framed as strengthening an established business;
  - stakeholder map and present/proposed responsibility boundaries;
  - complete collection lifecycle from portfolio intake through contact, payment evidence, reconciliation, cessation, reporting and improvement;
  - platform layers, modules, data authority, integrations, security, resilience and auditability;
  - managed-collection operating model, training, supervision, quality and escalation;
  - regulatory-readiness controls expressed as design requirements, not legal conclusions;
  - Sifututor capability proof and honest reuse/adaptation/new-build boundaries;
  - outcome model, KPI dictionary, baseline, pilot and commercial principles;
  - TEKUN Corporation historical milestones and a separate proposed implementation roadmap;
  - long-term partnership durability, TEKUN ownership, data portability and transition readiness;
  - discovery questions, assumptions, claim register, glossary and safe-use guidance.
- **Must not include:**
  - any claim that TEKUN Corporation is broken, lacks collection capability or has appointed us;
  - any named incumbent provider, fee, commission, portfolio size, baseline or system not confirmed by TEKUN Corporation;
  - guaranteed recovery, collection-rate, profit or revenue improvement;
  - a conclusion about statutory applicability, registration status, data role or legal authority;
  - “fully compliant,” certification or regulatory-approval claims;
  - coercive lock-in language or design, including data hostage and undocumented proprietary dependency;
  - credentials, customer data, infrastructure identifiers, security weaknesses, access methods or exploitable topology;
  - volatile development progress, completion percentages, sprint state or fragile delivery dates;
  - final pricing, contractual promises or implementation commitments before discovery and owner approval;
  - a prescribed slide order or creative direction that limits internal presentation teams.
- **Current facts versus future direction:** Every substantive statement is labelled or written as established/publicly stated, proposed partnership design, future roadmap, illustrative option or discovery dependency.
- **Sensitive/confidential boundary:** The governed master is internal/confidential. A controlled stakeholder edition may include approved capabilities, architecture abstractions, operating model, public research and roadmap; commercial numbers, staffing, provider contracts, raw baselines, security specifics and legal advice remain restricted.
- **Named organisations and examples:** TEKUN Corporation is the direct prospective client. TEKUN Nasional is described only as parent and possible originating relationship where evidenced. Other principals are named only where public, relevant and reverified. Sifututor is a capability reference, not a claim of collection-industry performance.
