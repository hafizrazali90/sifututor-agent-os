## 2. Editorial System

### Reader ladder

Every substantial chapter uses a three-layer reader model:

1. **In one minute** — non-technical meaning.
2. **How it works** — people, process and system flow, including practical value.
3. **Control and evidence** — technical, regulatory, measurement and discovery depth where the subject requires it.

The labels need not appear mechanically in every short chapter; the ordering and depth must remain recognisable.

### Voice and terminology

- Use **TEKUN Corporation** or **TCorp** only after defining the abbreviation; do not shorten it to ambiguous “TEKUN” when the entity matters.
- Use **financing recipient**, **customer** or **customer in arrears** according to the underlying relationship; do not call repayable financing a grant.
- Use **collection** for payment follow-up and **recovery** only with a defined cohort/denominator.
- Define **principal**, **authoritative system**, **reconciliation**, **portfolio**, **promise to pay**, **hardship hold**, **multi-principal** and **transition readiness** before specialist use.
- Use “designed to enforce/support/evidence” rather than “guarantees compliance.”
- Use “improve the conditions for” or “measurable pathway to” rather than guarantee profit.

### Positive-truth standard

The document shows progress and potential without showing daily implementation weakness:

> Established strength → Connected capability → Controlled operation → Measurable value → Expansion leverage

Positive framing must not hide context that would materially change a reasonable reader's understanding. Unknown private facts become discovery questions, not invented answers.

### Citation style

- Claim IDs in the governed Markdown, with footnotes/endnotes for reader-facing editions.
- Public/company claims trace to the TEKUN research claim and source registers.
- Sifututor capability claims trace to the ecosystem sourcebook claim register.
- Legal text is paraphrased; quotations remain short and sourced.

## 4. Detailed Roadmap Design

The roadmap is capability-gated, not date-promised. Dates may be added only after discovery, regulatory pathway confirmation, portfolio access and resource approval.

| Horizon | Objective | Capabilities delivered | Dependencies | Business impact | Evidence gate before expansion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H0 — Align and baseline** | Establish shared truth and lawful operating boundary | Current-state map; portfolio segmentation; authority/RACI; provider/system map; regulatory/privacy role map; KPI definitions; pilot cohort; data-quality profile | TCorp access, counsel/compliance, anonymised evidence | Removes design ambiguity and creates a fair measurement basis | Signed discovery pack; accepted baseline and control requirements |
| **H1 — Trusted operating foundation** | Make one bounded portfolio visible, controlled and auditable | Tenant/principal structure; account/arrears core; identity/access; intake; case queues; interaction log; payment evidence; audit/export; monitoring; backup/restore plan | H0; secure data transfer; policy templates | One dependable operating record and management visibility | Data reconciliation accepted; access tests pass; restore demonstrated; users trained |
| **H2 — Controlled pilot operation** | Run authorised collection with enforceable customer-treatment controls | Verification gate; contact limits; notices; promises; disputes; complaints; hardship/legal holds; cessation; supervisor QA; representative records; pilot dashboards | H1; approved scripts/notices; staffing/authorisation; payment feed | Safer, more consistent execution and first TEKUN-specific results | Full cycle completed; agreed outcome movement; conduct guardrails within threshold; no unresolved critical control failure |
| **H3 — Integrated performance scale** | Extend proven operation across a principal or broader cohort | Automated principal feeds; reconciliation integration; approval workflows; principal reports; workforce/capacity planning; service governance; onboarding/offboarding playbook | H2 evidence; integration owners; commercial approval | More portfolio capacity, faster onboarding, clearer cost and performance | Principal accepts reporting; stable operations; SLA and reconciliation targets met; playbook tested |
| **H4 — Multi-principal growth** | Replicate the model across additional appointing organisations | Configurable policy by principal; portfolio templates; segregated portals/reports; cross-principal control monitoring; standard integration kit | H3 stability; new-principal agreements; data segregation proof | Ability to win and serve more principals without proportional reinvention | Each new principal passes onboarding, isolation, control and reconciliation acceptance |
| **H5 — Governed intelligence and expansion** | Turn accumulated evidence into better decisions and new service capacity | Cohort/strategy insight; quality analytics; explainable agent assistance; forecasting; advanced channel orchestration; optional new portfolio types | Sufficient clean history; human oversight; ADMP/legal review; proven controls | Better strategy, lower avoidable work and stronger growth proposition | Models/assistance validated, explainable, monitored and approved; transition readiness remains demonstrable |

### Roadmap rules

- An outcome improvement never overrides a customer-treatment or compliance gate.
- Pilot failure leads to adjust, re-baseline or stop—not automatic expansion.
- Every horizon keeps TEKUN ownership, documented configuration, usable data export and transition support current.
- New principals inherit a tested onboarding pattern but still receive their own policy, data, isolation and reporting acceptance.
- AI appears only after reliable data and controls; humans retain consequential authority.

## 7. Evidence Model

- **Reader-facing labels:** Established; Publicly stated; Proposed partnership design; Conditional on discovery/legal confirmation; Future roadmap; Illustrative option.
- **Source/evidence classes:** T1 owner-confirmed TEKUN fact; T2 TCorp first-party public source; T3 Malaysian primary authority; T4 supporting benchmark/research; S1 Sifututor owner decision; S2 internal architecture/system evidence; S3 point-in-time operating proof; I interpretation.
- **Claim-ID pattern:** Reuse `CLM-###` for TEKUN research and `CLM-*` Sifututor sourcebook IDs; create `PROP-###` for proposed design and `DEC-###` for owner decisions.
- **Volatile-claim rule:** Reverify regulatory status, officeholders, client counts, appointments, product/provider statements and Sifututor public metrics immediately before external output.
- **Contradictions:** Resolve using primary authority where possible; otherwise disclose in the internal register and exclude from external narrative.
- **Unknowns:** Write “not publicly disclosed” or “requires confirmation,” never “does not exist.”

## 8. Sensitive-Content Boundaries

- **Never include:** Customer-identifiable data, credentials, access instructions, security vulnerabilities, exact sensitive topology, legal advice, unverified accusations, guaranteed results or coercive-retention language.
- **Internal only:** Named providers/contracts, raw portfolio data, cost model, pricing, staffing model, detailed legal analysis, incident history, data-quality defects, open assumptions and negotiation strategy.
- **Owner approval required:** Revenue/collection figures, target percentages, commercial terms, client/provider names, product launch dates, legal-status wording and external distribution list.
- **Safe abstraction:** Roles and permission principles; data-flow purposes; platform layers; control families; KPI definitions; roadmap gates; TEKUN ownership and transition readiness.

## 9. Update Governance

- **Owner:** Hafiz / designated proposal owner.
- **Scheduled review:** Monthly during proposal/design; quarterly after stabilisation.
- **Milestone triggers:** Discovery, legal/compliance conclusion, pilot approval, architecture approval, commercial approval, pilot result, new principal, regulatory change.
- **Derived-format rule:** Regenerate HTML/PDF/DOCX from the approved Markdown master after every material source change; never edit derived files as separate truth.
