# ICAN UiTM partnership deck

Partnership discussion deck for the meeting with the Office of Industry,
Community and Alumni Network (ICAN) at Universiti Teknologi MARA, hosted by its
Division of Community and Sustainability Network (BJKK), on 9 September 2026 at
Rumah Alumni, Intekma Resort, UiTM Shah Alam.

Presenter on the deck: Hazman Shahrill, Partnerships and Business Development
Executive, Sifututor.

## What the deck says

Twelve slides: who we are, operating scale, the fit with BJKK's units, how
one placement works, three tracks (talent and income; practicum and knowledge
transfer; joint technology and artificial intelligence projects), safety, the
ask, and indicative next steps.

The ask is one first pilot on the track ICAN picks, with the other two tracks
as later options. Economics are stated openly: no fee to UiTM, families pay
the standard rates, tutors receive the published rate, tutors pay the RM100
commitment fee under the published terms, any practicum allowance to be
agreed.

## Facts used

Only the approved external figures: since 2018; RM25m+ paid to tutors and
asatizah (payout, never revenue); 20,000+ tutors and asatizah engaged; about
2,000 delivering monthly; 1,000,000+ teaching hours; 50,000+ students;
10,000+ classes a month and ~69 new requests a day (verified July 2026); the
public rate range RM9 to RM84 per hour. See the Koda memory
`learnest-operating-scale` for provenance.

The safety slide separates controls that run today from controls proposed for
the pilot. All data access for projects is described as de-identified and
approval-dependent.
