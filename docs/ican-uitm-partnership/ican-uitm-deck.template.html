How placements are protected

  • One named supervisor per student, with a weekly check-in
  • A written deliverable agreed in week one
  • Access to personal data limited to the role; none for most placements
  • Work is reviewed before anything reaches tutors or families
  • Strong performers may be invited to apply for tutoring or future openings; no placement guarantees a role
This follows the shape UiTM already uses for industry attachments: an industry supervisor, a defined scope, and faculty sign-off on what counts.
Placement roles reflect current Sifututor and Nakngaji work areas; supervisors are Sifututor staff.

Scoped student projects on real platform problems, supervised jointly, with results that may be published subject to joint approval. Artificial intelligence work follows human review before anything reaches a learner.

Project ideas to scope together

Community impact dashboard for ICANCohort, district and subject views for the Community Impact Lab, built on live data.
4
Safer learning conversationsDetecting risky messages between tutors and families, with a person deciding every case.
Governance for any project: ethics review, a lawful basis or consent, data minimisation and documented de-identification, bias and safeguarding review, controlled research access, human review before release, and no autonomous action toward any learner. Aligned with the National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics (2024). Ideas are illustrative until scoped.

Children and families are involved, so safety is designed in, not added later.

Some controls already run on the platform. The pilot adds the safeguards a university will expect, agreed with ICAN. We would welcome guidance from CyberSecurity Malaysia on child online safety.

1In place today, before a family is confirmed

  • Phone and identity records checked
  • Screening quiz on teaching procedure and tajwid for Nakngaji applicants
  • Approval by the Tutor Experience team before any family is confirmed
  • Tutors can be suspended or removed, with the record kept

2In place today, during classes

  • Attendance recorded per class
  • Payment through the platform, monthly, for classes delivered
  • A staffed Tutor Experience support line for tutors and families
  • Changes and cancellations go through the platform, so there is a trail

3Proposed for the pilot, with ICAN

  • Guardian consent and communication boundaries for UiTM tutors
  • Home-visit rules, incident escalation and complaint response times
  • A safeguarding briefing before the first class
  • Role-limited access for placements, ending when the placement ends
  • Data handling for the pilot documented under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010
