# Document Acceptance Checklist — <document>

## Direction And Scope

- [ ] The document contract and blueprint are approved.
- [ ] Audience, purpose, central story and non-goals remain intact.
- [ ] Current capability, history, strategy, interpretation and illustration are distinct.
- [ ] Confidentiality and sharing boundaries are respected.

## Research And Evidence

- [ ] Internal truth was checked against current authoritative sources.
- [ ] Deep online research was completed when required by the lane.
- [ ] Every high-impact claim is traceable to the claim/source register.
- [ ] Material contradictions are resolved or disclosed.
- [ ] Volatile claims were reverified after drafting.
- [ ] Unsupported claims were removed.

## Content And Usability

- [ ] Required chapters/sections are complete.
- [ ] Technical and non-technical reader paths work.
- [ ] Terms are defined before specialist use.
- [ ] Visuals materially improve understanding.
- [ ] Positive framing does not omit meaning-changing context.

## Outputs

- [ ] Governed Markdown master is current.
- [ ] Required HTML/PDF/DOCX outputs were regenerated from the master.
- [ ] Output metadata is correct.
- [ ] Every diagram renders and is readable.
- [ ] Representative dense pages, tables, page breaks and final pages were visually inspected.

## Assurance

- [ ] Structural and link checks pass.
- [ ] Claim-register reconciliation passes.
- [ ] Sensitive-content scan passes.
- [ ] Output completeness checks pass.
- [ ] Blocking findings remaining: 0.
- [ ] Material findings remaining: 0.
- [ ] Minor findings are corrected or explicitly accepted.
