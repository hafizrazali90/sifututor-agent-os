## 7. Discovery-only material - used, then discarded as evidence

Search-engine result summaries were used to **locate** primary sources. In every
case the underlying source was then opened and read. Per the research method,
**no claim in this pack rests on a search snippet.**

Two snippet errors were detected and corrected against primary sources:

| Snippet claim | Primary source | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The CCA transition period is "six months ending 1 June 2026" | SRC-001 s.135(1), SRC-003, SRC-004 | The six months **begins** 1 June 2026 |
| CGAP/MicroRate standards are "explicit that rescheduled and restructured loans belong in the PAR numerator regardless of payment status" | SRC-029 items B3 and R11 | B3 **excludes** restructured and rescheduled loans; R11's explanation treats inclusion as a disclosure choice |

A third snippet-level ambiguity was noted and handled: the MOF field code 221105
was described by commercial MOF-registration agency websites as *Pemungut
Hutang/Penghantar Notis*. **That reading is not relied upon.** The pack cites only
TCorp's own first-party statement (SRC-016) that it is registered under code
221105 for *Perkhidmatan Pemungut Hutang*.
