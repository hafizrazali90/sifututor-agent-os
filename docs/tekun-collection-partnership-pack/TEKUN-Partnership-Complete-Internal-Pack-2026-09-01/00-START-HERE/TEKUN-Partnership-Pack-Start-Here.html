1. The partnership in one minute

TEKUN Corporation is an established collection operator. The proposed partnership would combine:

  1. TEKUN Corporation's relationships, policy and authority;
  2. a TEKUN Corporation-owned and governed collection platform;
  3. our team's platform design, build, operation, maintenance and authorised managed collection capability; and
  4. shared measurement, customer-treatment controls and continuous improvement.

The immediate decision is not to approve a price, launch date or guaranteed collection result. It is to authorise a governed discovery and pilot co-design programme, then decide whether to implement and expand based on evidence.

8. External circulation gates

Before sending the executive deck or controlled proposal outside the internal team:

  1. confirm the proposing legal entity and its relationship to the Sifututor operating capability;
  2. add the authorised proposal contact;
  3. confirm the intended recipients and confidentiality treatment;
  4. reverify volatile public and regulatory sources;
  5. obtain appropriate legal, compliance and privacy review for portfolio-specific statements;
  6. keep the internal deck, sourcebook, research pack and governance files out of the external package;
  7. do not add pricing, targets, guarantees or implementation commitments without evidence and owner approval.

9. Package structure

Two compiled ZIP files accompany this guide:

  • Complete Internal Pack: every reader-facing document, the research evidence and the governance material. This is the internal knowledge base.
  • Controlled External Review Pack: only the executive presentation, controlled proposal and external read-me. This is the candidate pack for authorised stakeholder review after the gates above are completed.

Rendering scripts, raw diagram-production files and temporary QA artifacts are intentionally excluded from the reader bundles. They remain in the workspace for future document maintenance.
