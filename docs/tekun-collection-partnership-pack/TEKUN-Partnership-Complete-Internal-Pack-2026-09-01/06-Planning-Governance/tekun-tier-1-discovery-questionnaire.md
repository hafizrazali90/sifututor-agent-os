# TEKUN Corporation Tier-1 Discovery Questionnaire

**Status:** Proposed meeting guide, not yet sent.

**Purpose:** Establish the minimum facts needed to design a credible platform, operating pilot and commercial structure without assuming TEKUN Corporation's current arrangements.

## 1. Recommended opening

> We recognise that TEKUN Corporation has operated credit management and recovery since 2015 and already serves multiple appointing organisations. Our purpose is not to assume what needs replacing. We would first like to understand your current model, priorities and regulatory pathway so any proposed platform and managed service strengthens what you have already built.

## 2. The 24 Tier-1 questions

### A. Direction and success

1. What outcomes does TEKUN Corporation most want to improve over the next 12 to 24 months, in priority order?
2. What is the main constraint today: portfolio capacity, contactability, data, process consistency, systems, people, compliance evidence, reconciliation or cost?
3. What would make a partnership visibly successful to management, the board and TEKUN Corporation's appointing organisations?

### B. Regulatory and decision authority

4. Which regulatory pathway does TEKUN Corporation's legal and compliance view consider applicable to each portfolio type, and what is the current authorisation, application or declaration status under that pathway?
5. Which collection activities could a delivery partner perform, and would partner personnel act as TEKUN Corporation representatives or require separate authorisation/registration?
6. Who holds authority for settlement, restructure, discount, write-off, legal escalation and account closure: TEKUN Corporation or each principal?
7. Which complaints, hardship, conduct-monitoring and regulatory-reporting processes must the solution support from day one?

### C. Principals and portfolios

8. Which organisations currently have active portfolios with TEKUN Corporation, and is the publicly stated figure of around 30 still current?
9. What financing or account types are assigned by each active principal?
10. What are the current account count, outstanding value, arrears/ageing distribution, geographic distribution and history depth by principal?
11. How are accounts assigned to TEKUN Corporation, updated, suspended, returned and closed?

### D. Current operating model and providers

12. Which collection functions are performed by TEKUN Corporation staff today: call centre, digital messaging, letters, field activity, tracing, legal coordination, reconciliation and reporting?
13. Does TEKUN Corporation engage any external provider for collection operations, people, technology, channels, data, payments, field work or legal recovery?
14. If providers exist, what does each one do, how is work allocated, how is performance governed, and when do the relevant contracts renew or expire?
15. What current systems support case work, dialling/communications, notices, payments, reconciliation, complaints and reporting?

### E. Customer treatment and operational control

16. How are work queues and treatment strategies created and approved for each principal and arrears stage?
17. How are identity verification, contact timing/frequency, notice delivery, consent, promises to pay and cessation currently recorded and evidenced?
18. How are complaints, disputes, hardship, vulnerable customers, deceased/bankrupt customers and legal holds handled?
19. How are agents trained, authorised, supervised, quality-checked and assessed for continuing competency?

### F. Money flow, data and integrations

20. Where do customers pay, who issues acknowledgement or receipt, and what event is authoritative for stopping collection?
21. Who reconciles payments to accounts, in what system and cycle, and what volume/ageing of exceptions exists?
22. What data is received from each principal, in which format and frequency, and what known completeness or quality issues affect collection?
23. Who is controller, processor or other accountable party for each data flow, and what hosting, access, retention, breach, export and audit requirements apply?

### G. Pilot and commercial readiness

24. Which bounded portfolio or arrears cohort would be safe and meaningful for a pilot, what historical baseline is available, and which outcome plus customer-treatment guardrails should determine whether it expands?

## 3. Requested pre-read evidence

Request only after TEKUN Corporation agrees the discovery scope and secure transfer method:

- active principal and portfolio summary;
- anonymised account/ageing distribution and 6–12 months of performance;
- current process map and authority matrix;
- system and integration inventory;
- sample assignment file, statement, notice, receipt and principal report;
- provider scope/contract summary where applicable;
- complaints, hardship and conduct-control summary;
- reconciliation process and exception-ageing summary;
- regulatory pathway/readiness summary approved for discussion;
- security, privacy, retention, backup and incident-control summary.

Do not request raw customer personal data during initial discovery.

## 5. Discovery output and decision gate

Discovery is complete enough for solution design only when the parties can jointly draw:

1. the current end-to-end collection lifecycle;
2. stakeholder and authority boundaries;
3. portfolio and principal segmentation;
4. system, data and payment flows;
5. confirmed regulatory and privacy responsibility map;
6. agreed baseline definitions and pilot cohort;
7. provider/integration/transition constraints;
8. a prioritised outcome and guardrail scorecard.

Pricing and performance promises remain deferred until these eight outputs are validated.
