# Why this opportunity matters now

## The environment is changing

- Malaysia's consumer-credit framework now places greater emphasis on authorisation, conduct, evidence and complaints handling.
- Customer treatment, identity verification, contact discipline and payment evidence must be designed into daily work.
- Different portfolios may follow different legal and operating pathways.

## The operating opportunity

- Connect portfolio, customer, interaction, payment and outcome information.
- Make authorised action easier to perform consistently and easier to prove.
- Create a reusable foundation for additional principals and portfolios.

**Important:** Actual regulatory applicability must be confirmed portfolio by portfolio.

<!-- layout: lifecycle -->
# A complete collection operating loop

**Intake and validate → Segment → Assign → Notify → Verify → Engage → Outcome branch: promise, protect, payment route or approved escalation → Reconcile → Cease/close → Improve**

Every stage has:

- a named owner;
- required information;
- an allowed next action;
- customer-treatment and authority controls;
- an auditable event and outcome.

Protected branches handle disputes, hardship, vulnerability, complaints and legal holds without losing the core record.

1. **Experience:** agent, supervisor, management, compliance and principal views.
2. **Authoritative portfolio:** principal, portfolio, customer/account, case and policy records.
3. **Controlled interaction:** queues, notices, contact, promises, complaints and holds.

# The solution combines platform and people

## Managed operating capability

- Authorised representatives perform approved work.
- Supervisors manage queues, quality, exceptions and capacity.
- Finance validates payment evidence and reconciliation.
- Compliance and privacy teams maintain independent oversight.
- Platform operations protect availability, change and recovery.
- Product and improvement teams convert evidence into better workflows.

Headcount, channel mix, location and service hours remain discovery and costing decisions.

<!-- layout: guardrails -->
# Customer treatment is part of the operating system

- Verify identity before discussing an account.
- Enforce approved contact windows, frequency and channel rules.
- Use controlled notices, scripts and evidence.
- Stop or redirect activity for settlement, dispute, hardship, vulnerability or legal hold.
- Keep complaints accessible and traceable.
- Representatives never receive customer money.
- Preserve human authority for consequential outcomes.

Final controls must follow confirmed portfolio applicability and TEKUN-approved policy.

<!-- layout: pilot -->
# Prove value safely through a bounded pilot

## A fair pilot needs

- one principal or one defined cohort;
- a written baseline and comparable history;
- agreed outcomes and customer-treatment guardrails;
- fixed measurement definitions and exclusions;
- weekly operating review and monthly governance review;
- explicit expand, adjust, re-baseline or stop decisions.

The pilot succeeds only when performance, conduct, reconciliation and platform-health evidence are all acceptable.
