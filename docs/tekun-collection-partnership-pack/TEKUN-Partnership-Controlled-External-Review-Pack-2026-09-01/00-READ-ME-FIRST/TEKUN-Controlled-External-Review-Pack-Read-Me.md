## The decision being requested

Authorise a governed discovery and pilot co-design programme. Full implementation should be considered only after authority, customer-treatment, evidence and commercial gates are satisfied.

## Important boundaries

- TEKUN Corporation is presented as an established collection operator, not an organisation starting from zero.
- The dedicated platform and managed operating model are proposed capabilities, not current TEKUN Corporation systems.
- No price, guaranteed recovery improvement, profit promise, final launch date or portfolio-specific legal conclusion is included.
- Final responsibilities, data roles, regulatory applicability, portfolio scope and commercial terms require confirmation.
- This pack does not identify or make assumptions about any incumbent third-party provider.
- The proposing legal entity, authorised contact and intended recipient list must be confirmed before formal circulation.
