Tutor Approval vs Tutor Activation

Use this guide when CX Support needs to approve an Unverified tutor for one request while TX continues verification and commitment fee follow-up in Ripple Tutor Experience.

Print Guide Download PDF

Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version

  • First confirm that Ripple shows Verification: Approved and the tutor remains Unverified only because the commitment fee is unpaid.
  • CX Support can then approve the tutor for one specific Tutor Request.
  • The tutor can work and be paid normally for that approved request, but remains globally Unverified and blocked from other requests.
  • TX uses Ripple Tutor Experience to complete verification and commitment fee follow-up.
  • Never manually change the tutor to Active or Verified. When payment settles and verification remains approved, SIMS updates the account automatically.

1. What changed

Approval and activation are now separate

Previously, staff sometimes changed a tutor from Unverified to Active in SIMS so the tutor could be accepted on a request. That mixed two different decisions. CX Support owns the request decision. TX owns verification and commitment fee follow-up. SIMS owns the final account transition after valid payment settlement.

CX Support: approve for request

First confirm Ripple Verification is Approved. Then use the SIMS Tutor Request approval action. The exception applies only to that exact request.

TX: complete verification and fee follow-up

Use Ripple Tutor Experience to correct allowed information, approve completed verification, share the FIUU link, or submit offline evidence.

Finance: confirm payment evidence

A different Finance reviewer confirms or rejects offline evidence. FIUU settlement needs no separate Finance confirmation.

Never manually change the account status

Request approval does not make the tutor globally Verified. SIMS applies the final transition only after valid settlement while verification remains approved.

3. Daily workflow

What each team should do

1

Confirm TX has approved the tutor verification

Open the tutor in Ripple Tutor Experience. Continue only when Verification shows Approved and the account remains Unverified because the commitment fee is unpaid. If verification is not Approved, stop and ask TX to complete the review.

2

CX Support approves the tutor for the request

Review suitability for the parent request. If suitable, click Approve in the SIMS Tutor Request workflow. Select a truthful reason, add a clear staff note, confirm the acknowledgement, and choose Approve Tutor for This Request.

3

Keep the tutor account Unverified while the fee is unpaid

The approval covers only the named Tutor Request. The tutor can work and be paid for that request. Other requests remain blocked unless CX Support repeats this approval workflow for each request. The exception and staff reason are not shown to the tutor or parent.

4

TX continues commitment fee follow-up

TX uses Ripple Tutor Experience to share the official FIUU link or submit offline evidence. For tutor payout deduction, submit evidence only after the full RM100 has already been deducted from processed tutor payments.

5

Let SIMS complete the account transition

When payment settles and verification remains approved, SIMS updates the tutor automatically. Staff must not manually change the tutor to Active or Verified.

4. Do and do not

Use the right action

Do

Approve the tutor for the request through the request workflow when CX Support decides the tutor is suitable.

Do not

Do not change the tutor to Active in SIMS just because CX Support needs to approve the tutor for a request.

Do

Let TX continue verification and commitment fee follow-up in Ripple while the tutor remains Unverified.

Do not

Do not manually set Active or Verified, and do not treat a planned or partial payout deduction as settled.

5. Troubleshooting

When staff are unsure

If this happens Meaning Do this
Tutor is Unverified but suitable for the request The exception is available only after TX has approved verification and the commitment fee remains unpaid. Check Ripple first. If Verification is Approved, CX Support may use the Tutor Request approval action. Keep the account Unverified.
Ripple Verification is not Approved The tutor is not ready for the request exception workflow. Do not approve the tutor for the request and do not change the account status. Ask TX to complete the verification review.
Staff wants to change Unverified to Active This is usually the old shortcut. Do not change the status. If the need is request approval, use the Tutor Request approval workflow. SIMS handles final verification automatically after settlement.
Commitment fee will be deducted from tutor payout This stays manual for now. Track the deduction manually. Submit deduction evidence only after the full RM100 has already been deducted from processed tutor payments.
Payment proof does not match SIMS state Finance owns the financial decision. Send it through the proper Finance review or FIUU exception path. Do not activate from a technical retry alone.
