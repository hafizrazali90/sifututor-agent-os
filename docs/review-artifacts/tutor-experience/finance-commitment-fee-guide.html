Finance & Accounting: Tutor Commitment Fee Review

Use this guide to independently review offline payments, confirm completed tutor payout deductions, and resolve FIUU payment exceptions without manually changing tutor status.

Start Review

Internal staff guide · Updated 27 July 2026 · Test screenshots only

The Short Version

  • This guide covers tutor commitment fees only. It does not cover parent commitment fees.
  • Finance decides whether submitted payment evidence is valid. TX submits evidence but cannot confirm its own submission.
  • For offline payments, match the full fee amount shown, date, reference, receiving destination, and proof before confirming.
  • For payout deductions, confirm only after the full fee amount shown was already deducted from processed tutor payments. The current amount is RM100.
  • A FIUU status retry only checks evidence. It cannot approve, settle, refund, or activate a tutor.
  • Never manually change a tutor to Active or Verified. SIMS owns settlement and activation.

Ownership

One Submission, One Independent Decision

The separation of duties protects Finance and the tutor. The person who prepares evidence is not the person who confirms that money was received or recovered.

TX

Submits Evidence

Uploads offline payment proof or records evidence that a full payout deduction has already happened.

Finance

Verifies and Decides

Independently checks the proof, records a reason, then confirms or rejects the evidence.

SIMS

Settles and Activates

Records the official result and changes the tutor to Verified only when payment is valid, verification remains Approved, and the account is otherwise eligible.

Decision Map

Choose the Correct Review Path

Start with how the commitment fee was handled. Do not treat every case as an ordinary payment.

Ripple

Offline Payment

Bank transfer, cash, cheque, or other offline-payment proof submitted by TX.

  1. Open Offline Payment Review.
  2. View the proof and tutor details.
  3. Match amount, date, account, and reference.
  4. Confirm or reject with a reason.

Confirm only when: the full fee amount shown is independently matched.

Ripple

Tutor Payout Deduction

Evidence that the full fee amount shown was already recovered from processed tutor payments.

  1. View the deduction proof and batch reference.
  2. Check the processed payout record.
  3. Confirm the full fee amount shown was already deducted.
  4. Confirm evidence or reject it.

Do not confirm: planned, future, or partial deductions.

SIMS Operations Centre

FIUU Payment Exception

A verified FIUU response does not match the saved payment state.

  1. Review the order and exception reason.
  2. Check FIUU evidence and tutor eligibility.
  3. Select the permitted resolution: confirm paid, mark refund required, or record duplicate for a transaction conflict.
  4. Record a clear decision reason.

Remember: marking refund required does not refund FIUU.

Path 1 · Ripple

Review an Offline Payment

Use the Finance queue, not the tutor verification worklist. Each card shows who submitted the proof and the payment details Finance must verify.

1

Open the Finance Queue

In Ripple, choose Tutor Experience → Offline payment review, then start with Pending.

Check the submitterYou cannot confirm evidence you submitted.
Check all four fieldsAmount, date, receiving account, and reference must match.
2

Record the Independent Decision

A decision reason is required. Write what was matched, not only “checked” or “okay”.

Good reason“Matched Maybank statement. Full fee amount, date, and reference confirmed.”
Reject when uncertainMissing, unreadable, mismatched, or duplicate proof must not be confirmed.

Path 2 · Ripple

Review Tutor Payout Deduction Evidence

This remains a manual Finance process. The queue records evidence only after the deduction has already happened in a processed tutor payment.

1

Check the Payout Batch and Proof

Confirm that the reference points to a processed payout and the full fee amount shown was recovered.

Required evidenceProcessed date, payout batch reference, deduction proof, and a clear note.
Full amount onlyA planned or partial deduction does not settle the fee.
2

Confirm What Already Happened

The button confirms evidence of a completed deduction. It does not perform the deduction.

Good reason“Matched payout batch 001 and proof. Full fee deduction confirmed.”
Reject planned deductionsFinance must first complete the manual deduction outside this review screen.

Path 3 · SIMS Operations Centre

Resolve a FIUU Payment Exception

In SIMS, choose Operations Centre → FIUU Payment Exceptions, then select Review FIUU exception. Use this path when FIUU evidence and the saved SIMS state do not agree. Ripple’s technical status refresh cannot make the financial decision.

1

Review the Exception Before Selecting a Resolution

Confirm paid only when the payment is verified and the tutor is currently eligible.

Confirm paidUse only when verified FIUU evidence supports settlement now.
Record duplicateFor a transaction conflict only, use this when the same tutor fee and FIUU transaction are already recorded as paid.
Refund requiredThis records the decision only. Complete the authorised refund outside SIMS, then update the case.

Before Confirming

Use the Finance Checklist

Offline Payment

  • ✓The amount matches the full fee amount displayed in the queue. The current amount is RM100.
  • ✓The payment date matches the attached evidence.
  • ✓The recorded receiving account or collection destination is correct.
  • ✓The payment reference, receipt number, or cheque number is visible and not duplicated.
  • ✓You did not submit this proof.

Payout Deduction

  • ✓The tutor payment was already processed.
  • ✓The full fee amount displayed in the queue was already deducted.
  • ✓The batch reference matches the proof.
  • ✓The note explains where the deduction appears.
  • ✓You did not submit this evidence.

Safety Rules

Never Do These

Do not self-confirmThe evidence submitter must not approve the same evidence.
Do not confirm a planA future or partial payout deduction is not completed evidence.
Do not request double paymentIf FIUU status is unclear, review the exception before asking the tutor to pay again.
Do not activate manuallyNever change the tutor to Active or Verified to bypass settlement.
Do not assume a retry settles moneyTechnical refresh only checks verified FIUU evidence.
Do not promise a refund is complete“Refund required” records a decision only. Complete the authorised refund outside SIMS, then update the case.

After the Decision

Understand the Result

Decision or Situation
Finance Meaning
System Result
Confirm payment
The full payment was independently verified.
SIMS settles the fee and changes the tutor to Verified only if verification remains Approved and the account is otherwise eligible.
Confirm deduction evidence
The full fee amount shown was already recovered from processed tutor payments.
SIMS settles the fee and applies the same Approved-verification and account-eligibility checks.
Reject evidence
The proof is missing, incorrect, duplicated, partial, or cannot be matched.
The fee remains unsettled and TX must correct or replace the evidence.
Record duplicate
The same tutor fee and FIUU transaction are already recorded as paid.
SIMS voids the duplicate attempt. It does not settle the fee a second time.
Mark refund required
The FIUU transaction should not settle the tutor fee.
SIMS records the decision. Finance must complete the authorised FIUU refund outside SIMS, then update the case.
Offline payment confirmed during suspension
The fee can be settled, but activation remains blocked.
SIMS records the offline settlement and leaves the tutor suspended. A FIUU “Confirm paid” resolution requires current eligibility.
